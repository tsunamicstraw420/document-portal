    MASTER STYLE PROMPT (FIRST)
    An antique Victorian scientific plate brought to life as a richly detailed hand-
    painted 3D diorama, in the style of a 19th-century engraving and anatomy-
    illustration collage: aged parchment paper, pinned engraved diagrams and botanical
    sketches in the background, brass gears, magnifying glasses and apothecary glass,
    tiny top-hatted Victorian gentlemen for scale.



    CLOSING PROMPT (LAST)
    Vertical composition 9:16, deep focus, subject centred with breathing room top and
    bottom for captions. Palette: parchment cream, dusty powder blue, copper, burnt
    orange and bronze-black, soft warm museum light, fine engraving cross-hatching
    texture. No readable text, no letters, no numbers except on clock faces, no logos,
    no watermark.


The ten scenes
#       SCENE (GOES BETWEEN THE TWO BLOCKS)

1       A giant steaming porcelain coffee cup rebuilt as a brass steam boiler, pipes feeding up into a
        large engraved human brain that works like a furnace; tiny top-hatted men shovel coffee beans
        into its firebox. Behind it, a bold burnt-orange and cream sunburst radiating from the centre,
        crows perched on the pipes.
2       Cross-section of an engraved brain drawn like a canal city of brass locks and waterways; a
        coffee-dark flood bursts through a small brass dam at the top, tiny engineers in waistcoats
        running along the canal walls, a crow taking off.
3       A large antique brass pressure gauge mounted on an engraved brain, its needle deep in the red
        zone; beneath it a tray of porcelain teacups on saucers and a tiny gentleman clutching his top
        hat.

6    A tall carved wooden cabinet of curiosities with its doors swung open; [PRODUCT] stands on a

Video prompts
Motion first, style reminder second, negatives last. Each line is what moves in that plate.

     VIDEO PROMPT FORMAT
     [motion line]. antique Victorian scientific plate brought to life as a hand-painted
     3d diorama, 19th-century engraving and anatomy-illustration collage, aged parchment,
     brass and copper instruments, tiny top-hatted gentlemen and lab-coated scientists,
     crows and robins, botanical sketches, deep focus, gentle painterly motion. no
     readable text, no morphing hands.


The ten motion lines
#        MOTION LINE

1        slow push-in toward the cup-boiler as steam puffs rhythmically from its pipes, the tiny men
         shovel beans, the brain-furnace glows brighter and the crows ruffle their feathers.
2        tilt down following the dark coffee flood for a moment, then the camera accelerates into a fast
         diving push-in, plunging into the swirling dark coffee flood until the swirl fills the whole frame and
         resolves into a round brass gauge dial.
3        static close shot with a subtle camera shake: the gauge needle trembles hard in the red, the
         teacups rattle on their saucers, the tiny gentleman wobbles and holds his hat.
4        slow push-in on the clockwork brain as its gears grind slower and slower to a stop, the tiny
         worker slumps further on his lever, the clock pendulum swings lazily.
5        orbit around the turning cup carousel for a moment, then the camera accelerates into a fast
         diving push-in over the exhausted tiny man and down into his porcelain coffee cup, the swirl of
         coffee filling the frame and resolving into your product on a brass pedestal, warmly lit.
6        slow push-in toward the product on its pedestal as warm light blooms across the shelves, the
         scientists lean closer with their magnifying glasses, the robin hops.
7        slow push-in as the scale settles, beans trickle off the heavy pan, the tiny scientist peers through
         his magnifying glass and nods.
8        slow crane up across the six glass cloches as soft light glints on the glass, the mushrooms gently
         breathe spores, the robins hop between cloches.
9        steady crane up following the brain balloon for a moment, then the camera accelerates into a fast
         diving push-in toward the brain balloon until its engraved folds fill the whole frame and bloom
         with flowers and turning gears.
10       slow pull-back revealing the blooming clockwork brain as its gears turn briskly, flowers open, the
         tiny workers bustle and the clock pendulum swings.
