Podcast Channel — YouTube Production & Growth SOP

Purpose: A step-by-step reference Claude (and the team) can follow when producing, packaging, and publishing a podcast episode on YouTube. Built from on-platform creator tutorials and tuned for a long-form podcast. Shortcut tactics that push against YouTube's guidelines are intentionally excluded (see bottom).

How to use: When helping produce or publish an episode, walk the workflow in order. Treat the "Per-episode checklist" as the definition of done. Apply the "Core principles" as the reasoning behind each step.

Core principles (the rules to optimize for)

  • Topic platform, not a subscriber platform. Each episode competes with every other video on its topic, not just for our subscribers.
  • Viewer satisfaction is the signal. Watch time, likes, comments, shares — and especially people returning for another episode.
  • Each episode is judged on its own performance, not channel history. One strong episode can break out.
  • Subscribers matter mostly in the first ~2 hours. After that, YouTube pushes the episode to its own topic audience or lets it fade.
  • The target is YouTube's own topic audience, not just existing followers.

Per-episode production workflow

1. Topic & keyword (before recording)

  • [ ] Pick a topic inside our one core niche (don't drift between unrelated topics).
  • [ ] Research the target keyword on YouTube — the phrase our listener would actually search.
  • [ ] Check YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → "What your audience watches" for topics the audience already wants; plan a higher-value version.
  • [ ] Favor evergreen, search-friendly angles: How to… / Why… / Best way to…

2. Structure for retention (recording & edit)

  • [ ] Target 8–10+ minutes of video (long-form is where a podcast wins watch hours).
  • [ ] Open with a strong hook; use open loops (tease what's coming) and storytelling.
  • [ ] Add visual interest and sound cues; keep on-camera delivery vibrant.

3. Packaging (before upload)

  • [ ] Rename the export file to include the target keyword (YouTube reads the file name, not just the title).
  • [ ] Write a search + curiosity title (How to / Why / Best way to, plus a curiosity twist).
  • [ ] Design a thumbnail that beats the ones already ranking for the title — search it, compare, redo if ours wouldn't win the click.

4. Upload settings (at publish)

  • [ ] Allow embedding is ON (Studio → video Details → Show more).
  • [ ] Notify subscribers checked — only once past ~1,000 subscribers.
  • [ ] Add end screens and cards pointing to related episodes.
  • [ ] Add a playlist link in the description; add the episode to an optimized playlist.
  • [ ] Upload defaults applied (disclaimer, social links, description template).
  • [ ] Set/refresh the Featured video (Spotlight) for returning subscribers and the channel trailer (Customization → Home tab) to a high-value episode.

5. Post-publish monitoring (48h and ongoing)

Check Studio → Analytics for these "about-to-be-pushed" signals and act:

SignalWhereAction
Impressions rising before viewsContent tabImprove the thumbnail
More Suggested / Browse trafficHow viewers find youPublish a follow-up on the same value
Non-subscriber views increasingAudience tabMake the intro friendlier to spark comments
Avg view duration ticking upOverviewStay consistent — don't change style/niche
Old episodes climbingContent tabBuild a playlist; add end screens + cards

Do NOT (growth-killers)

  • No manipulative multi-channel engagement (using other channels on the same device/IP to like, comment, or watch our own videos) — YouTube filters it and distorts analytics.
  • Don't switch the channel's niche. New direction = new channel.
  • At momentum, don't delete episodes or change style/niche.
  • Don't farm watch hours (self-watching across accounts, external-site streaming) — YouTube strips them and may delete videos.

Shorts policy

  • Use shorts only as clips drawn from a full episode, each with a clear CTA back to the episode.
  • Keep them sparse — don't train a shorts-only audience. Shorts watch time does not count toward monetization.

Per-episode checklist (copy per episode)

  • [ ] Target keyword researched
  • [ ] Export file renamed with the keyword
  • [ ] Search + curiosity title written
  • [ ] Thumbnail beats the ranking competition
  • [ ] Episode 8–10+ min with hook, story, and retention devices
  • [ ] End screens + cards + playlist link added
  • [ ] Embedding on; notify subscribers (if past 1k subs)
  • [ ] Featured (Spotlight) episode + channel trailer set/refreshed
  • [ ] Signals checked at 48h and acted on
