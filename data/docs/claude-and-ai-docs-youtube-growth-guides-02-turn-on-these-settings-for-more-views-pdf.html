Turn On These 4 Settings to Get More Views
       The channel settings the creator credits for 0 → 1,000 subs in 21 days



      Video: “Turn ON These Settings to Get More Views (Small Channels)”
      Creator: Chiamaka Okoli (Coach Chaka) • Length: 9:22 • Link: youtube.com/watch?v=xHDdBJDC8wg




    What this guide covers
    The creator says changing a few overlooked settings took a faceless channel from zero to 1,000+
    subscribers in 21 days. Each setting does a specific job: pulling in external views, bringing subscribers
    back, and helping the algorithm understand your niche. This guide walks through all four (three core
    settings plus a time-saving bonus) with the exact clicks.

1    Allow embedding — unlock views from outside YouTube.
2    Notify subscribers — bring existing subscribers back to each upload.
3    Channel keywords — tell the algorithm your niche and who to serve.
4    Upload defaults (bonus) — save time and optimize every upload automatically.


                   SETTING 1
           1       Allow embedding (external views)                                                 ■ 1:35–3:24


      When embedding is on, others can pin your video on their websites and blogs, and those visitors can
      flow to your channel — giving you views from Google and other external sources.

      1.       In YouTube Studio, open a video and click Details.
      2.       Scroll down and click Show more.
      3.       Keep scrolling to Allow embedding and make sure the box is checked.



                   SETTING 2
           2       Notify subscribers (bring them back)                                             ■ 3:24–5:35


      This tells YouTube to notify subscribers when you post, so repeat viewers come back. The creator saw
      ~10x more views in the first hour after turning it on.

      1.       This is set while uploading a video — scroll down the upload screen.
      2.       Find Notify subscribers and check the box before publishing.
      ■ Her caveat: only turn this on once you have at least ~1,000 subscribers, so the algorithm doesn't judge new
      videos mainly on your small subscriber base's reaction.

                  SETTING 3
         3        Channel keywords (niche clarity)                                                              ■ 5:35–7:31


    Channel keywords are a main place the algorithm looks to understand your niche, build topical
    authority, and align you with search. Get these right and recommendations improve.

    1.       In Studio, go to Settings → Channel → find Channel keywords.
    2.       Add three kinds of keywords: a niche keyword, an audience-intent keyword, and a
             problem-based keyword.
    3.       Revisit and update these roughly every 6 months (a habit big creators keep).



                  SETTING 4 · BONUS
         4        Upload defaults (save time + optimize)                                                        ■ 7:31–8:58


    Set the things you add to every video once, and they apply automatically to future uploads.

    1.       In Studio, go to Settings → Upload defaults.
    2.       Pre-fill what you always add: disclaimer, social links, and a description template.
    3.       Add a link to one of your playlists here to help optimize the playlist and drive more views.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Allow embedding is checked on my videos
    ■ Notify subscribers ready to check at upload (once past ~1,000 subs)
    ■ Channel keywords set: niche + audience-intent + problem-based
    ■ Calendar reminder to review channel keywords in ~6 months
    ■ Upload defaults filled with disclaimer, socials, and a playlist link



    Bottom line: these are small, one-time settings, but together they open up external traffic, repeat viewers, and
    clearer niche signals — the mix the creator credits for rapid early growth.
