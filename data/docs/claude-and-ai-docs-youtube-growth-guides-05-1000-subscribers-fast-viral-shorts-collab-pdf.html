      Collabs
      Ride traffic YouTube is already pushing — the legal, approved way



     Video: “This Hack Can Get You 1,000 Subscribers Fast (Small Channel)”
     Creator: Chiamaka Okoli (Coach Chaka) • Length: 9:15 • Link: youtube.com/watch?v=hLN3kQBSZ84




  What this guide covers
  This strategy uses YouTube's own Collab / “Use this sound” feature on trending short videos to borrow
  traffic YouTube is already pushing. Because YouTube cross-promotes look-alike content to look-alike
  audiences, your reaction rides alongside the viral original. This guide covers finding the right short,
  recording your collab, and the three optimizations (title keywords, thumbnail, timing) that make it convert.

1 Find a qualifying viral short in your niche.
2 Record a collab/reaction the right way.
3 Optimize title, thumbnail, and timing, then post on a cadence.

                   STEP 1 · FIND
          1        Find the right viral short                                                     ■ 1:08–2:40


     You want a currently-viral short in your niche so the audience it brings actually matches your channel.

     1.       Open the YouTube app and search viral short videos (or a keyword in your niche).
     2.       Tap Shorts so only short videos show.
     3.       Pick a video that is 1 year old or newer (older ones won't ride current traffic).
     4.       Prefer high view counts and strong engagement, and make sure it's in your niche.

                  STEP 2 · RECORD
         2        Create your collab / reaction                                                   ■ 2:40–6:49


    YouTube approves this via the collab option, and these videos can still be monetized.

    1.       On the chosen short, tap the button that offers “Use this sound / Collab.” Its presence means the
             creator allows collaboration.
    2.       Watch the full video first so you know how to react to each moment.
    3.       Choose a layout — the viral video on top, you at the bottom works best (frees your hands to point
             and express).
    4.       Record with a tripod so the shot is steady, and be genuinely expressive.
    5.       Trim/edit if needed, then process the video.



                  STEP 3 · OPTIMIZE + POST
         3        Three optimizations and a cadence                                               ■ 6:49–8:57


    These three make YouTube route the original's traffic to you:

    1.       Title keywords: reuse the keywords from the viral video's title in your own title.
    2.       Thumbnail: shorts have thumbnails — tap the pencil icon and pick an intriguing frame (ideally
             matching the original's hook).
    3.       Timing: upload around the same time of day the original was posted, to catch the same active
             audience.
    4.       Post 2–4 times a day, using different viral videos in your niche (she suggests 50M+ view originals).
    5.       Once you pass 1,000 subscribers and engagement is strong, start creating your own original
             content.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Found a niche short, 1 year old or newer, with strong engagement
    ■ Used the Collab / Use-this-sound option
    ■ Watched the full original before reacting
    ■ Recorded steady on a tripod, expressive, good layout
    ■ Reused the original's title keywords
    ■ Set an intriguing shorts thumbnail
    ■ Matched the original's upload timing; posting 2-4x/day
