1,000 Subscribers Without Uploading: Collab +
      Auto-Subscribe Link
      Two hacks you can apply to your existing videos and channel



     Video: “This Hack Will Get You 1,000 Subscribers Fast Without Uploading New Videos”
     Creator: Chiamaka Okoli (Coach Chaka) • Length: 9:45 • Link: youtube.com/watch?v=NT_XgqQkK-0




  What this guide covers
  Two hacks that work on your old videos, so you don't have to upload anything new. First, YouTube's
  built-in collaboration feature exposes one of your existing videos to a bigger creator's audience. Second,
  an auto-subscribe link forces anyone who opens your channel to subscribe before doing anything else.
  This guide gives the exact clicks for both, plus how to get a big creator to actually accept your collab.

1 Hack 1 — collaborate an existing video with a bigger creator.
2 How to get the creator to accept (offer real value).
3 Hack 2 — create an auto-subscribe channel link.

                  HACK 1 · COLLAB
          1       Collaborate an old video with a bigger creator                                ■ 2:03–5:50


     When a bigger creator accepts, YouTube exposes your video to their audience.

     1.       In YouTube Studio, click Content and select the existing video you want to collaborate (choose one
              that fits the target creator's audience).
     2.       Click the pencil (edit) icon, scroll down and click Show more.
     3.       Find Collaboration and click Invite collaborator.
     4.       Search the creator's name, select them, and set Allow analytics view on or off as you prefer.
     5.       Click Create link, then Copy invite link, and Save.

                  HACK 1 · GET ACCEPTED
         2        Getting the big creator to say yes                                                        ■ 5:50–7:01


    The hard part isn't the link — it's approval. Offer value first.

    1.       Find the creator's contact: open their channel → More → email address (or reach them on
             WhatsApp / a platform you both use).
    2.       Send the invite link with a clear value offer — e.g. design a thumbnail for them, or edit one of their
             videos.
    3.       Without added value (or an existing relationship), expect most creators to decline.



                  HACK 2 · AUTO-SUBSCRIBE
         3        Create an auto-subscribe channel link                                                     ■ 7:15–9:28


    This link makes anyone who clicks it confirm a subscription before they can continue.

    1.       Get your channel link the right way: Studio → Customization → scroll to Channel URL under
             Profile and copy it.
    2.       In Google, search auto subscribe link generator and open the Social Video Plaza “free YouTube
             auto subscribe link” tool.
    3.       Paste your channel link and click Generate, then copy the new link.
    4.       Use Test it to confirm it shows the “Confirm channel subscription” prompt, then share that link
             where your traffic lands.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Picked an old video that fits a bigger creator's audience
    ■ Created and copied the collaboration invite link
    ■ Prepared a genuine value offer for the creator
    ■ Sent the invite via email / direct message
    ■ Copied my channel URL from Customization
    ■ Generated and tested an auto-subscribe link



    Bottom line: the collab borrows a bigger audience and the auto-subscribe link converts that attention — both
    applied to videos you already have.
