The Reaction-Short Strategy That Got a Student
      22,000 Subscribers
      Borrow viral traffic, then use the @mention secret to ride a big creator's searches



    Video: “How My Student Got 22,000 Subscribers as a Small Channel”
    Creator: Chiamaka Okoli (Coach Chaka) • Length: 11:10 • Link: youtube.com/watch?v=Yuq5-hxxI8I




  What this guide covers
  A refined version of the viral-short reaction strategy, credited with taking a student past 22,000 subscribers
  and multiple videos to 700k–900k views. You react to qualifying viral shorts in your niche, use a
  funny/intriguing frame as the thumbnail, copy the original's keywords and hashtags — and the key secret:
  @mention a big creator in your title and description so your video surfaces whenever people search that
  creator's name.

1 Qualify a viral short (views, comments, likes, recency).
2 Record an expressive reaction and pick a scroll-stopping thumbnail.
3 Optimize + the @mention secret to ride a big creator's search traffic.

                   STEP 1 · QUALIFY
          1        Find a qualifying viral short                                                  ■ 3:10–4:16


     Tighter criteria than a basic search — you want proof the short is genuinely viral and in your niche.

     1.       Open YouTube, search a keyword in your niche, and filter to Shorts.
     2.       Find a short with at least 1 million views.
     3.       Check the comments (aim for 1,000+), the likes (1,000+), and that it was uploaded recently.
     4.       Confirm it delivers value (teaches, motivates, entertains) and fits your niche.



                   STEP 2 · RECORD
          2        React well and pick the thumbnail                                              ■ 4:41–7:09


     Reactions that win aren't just reactions — they add value and a strong facial hook.

     1.       Watch the full original before reacting so your reaction lands.
     2.       Record with a tripod and be very expressive (clap, laugh, react big).
     3.       After recording, scrub to the most interesting / funniest moment and screenshot your reaction.
     4.       Use that frame as the short's thumbnail — a curious, funny face drives the click.

                 STEP 3 · OPTIMIZE + @MENTION
         3       Keywords, hashtags, and the @mention secret                                               ■ 7:24–10:25


    This is where the reach comes from:

    1.       Grab the keywords and hashtags the original creator used and put them in your title and
             description.
    2.       The secret: in your title (and/or description), @mention the big creator (type @ + their handle so
             it highlights in blue).
    3.       Because you mentioned them, your video will surface when people search that creator's name —
             huge if they have hundreds of thousands of subscribers.
    4.       Only use videos in your niche (random viral videos bring the wrong audience).
    5.       Alternate reaction videos with your own original content, and post ~4–5 a day.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Found a niche short: 1M+ views, 1,000+ comments, 1,000+ likes, recent
    ■ Watched the full original before reacting
    ■ Recorded expressive on a tripod
    ■ Set a funny/intriguing frame as the thumbnail
    ■ Copied the original's keywords and hashtags into title + description
    ■ @mentioned the big creator in the title/description
    ■ Alternating reactions with my own original videos



   Bottom line: the @mention is what makes this different from ordinary reaction videos — it plugs you into a big
   creator's name searches. Keep it in-niche so the subscribers you gain actually want your original content.
