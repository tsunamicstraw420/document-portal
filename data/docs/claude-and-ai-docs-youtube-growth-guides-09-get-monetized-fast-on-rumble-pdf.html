  What this guide covers
  This video points to Rumble as an alternative platform the creator says monetizes an account on sign-up
  — no subscriber or watch-hour threshold, a lower ($50) payout minimum, and AI content allowed. This
  guide walks the exact account setup, profile and first upload, and the topics she says perform best, so you
  can repurpose your YouTube content there.

                   STEP 1 · SIGN UP
          1        Create a free Rumble account                                                 ■ 3:06–5:05


     You can do this on phone or computer.

     1.       In Google, search Rumble and open the site; click Sign up.
     2.       Enter your email and date of birth, click Next, and verify you're human.
     3.       Choose a username (no spaces), select gender and country, check the box, and continue.
     4.       Wait for verification, then create a strong password (8+ chars, uppercase, lowercase, special
              character).
     5.       Enter the code emailed to you and click Next to finish.

                  STEP 2 · SET UP + UPLOAD
         2        Optimize your profile and post a video                                                      ■ 5:05–7:24


    Your dashboard shows the monetized status right away.

    1.       Open the dashboard to see monetization/earnings, then go to Account settings → Profile.
    2.       Add a profile photo and a description of your channel, and connect your socials.
    3.       To post, click Upload, add title, description, category, and thumbnail, then select your video file.
    4.       Click Upload and wait for “video upload complete.”



                  STEP 3 · WHAT TO POST
         3        Topics that earn, and repurposing                                                           ■ 7:24–8:58


    Her three hot topics and the fastest way to fill your channel:

    1.       Lean into news (especially US-audience news), politics, and music — the topics she shows
             pulling the most views.
    2.       Repurpose your YouTube videos with small tweaks so the same content earns on both platforms.
    3.       Stay consistent; you can use AI to adapt content (Rumble allows AI content).
    ■ Payout details she cites: $50 minimum withdrawal (vs YouTube's $100), and ~100 followers to cash out.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Created a Rumble account (username has no spaces)
    ■ Verified email with the code
    ■ Added profile photo, description, and connected socials
    ■ Uploaded a first video with title, description, category, thumbnail
    ■ Chose a lane: news / politics / music
    ■ Repurposed a YouTube video with small tweaks



    Bottom line: Rumble is pitched as a faster path to monetization while you build YouTube. Treat the “instant
    monetization” framing with healthy skepticism, verify current payout terms on Rumble directly, and use it to
    diversify rather than replace your channel.
