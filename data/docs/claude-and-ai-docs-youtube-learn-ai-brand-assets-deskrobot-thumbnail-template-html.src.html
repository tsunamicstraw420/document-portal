How to use this template

  • Click any text (the tag, the headline) and just type your own.
  • To color a word orange, wrap it: <span class="hl">WORD</span>. For aqua use class="aqua". (Edit in the HTML, or keep it simple and skip.)
  • Keep the headline to 3–5 words. If it runs onto 3 lines, shorten it — thumbnails are read at thumbnail size.
  • Export: screenshot just the cream box (it's exactly 1280×720). On Mac: Shift+Cmd+4, then drag the box — or open this file in Chrome, right-click the box → Inspect, and screenshot the #thumb node.
  • Swap the character pose by replacing the image with any pose from your pose sheet (cut it out first).
