  What this guide covers
  If you are a small creator putting in real work but getting fewer than ~500 views per video, the video
  argues that the fastest lever is YouTube’s recommendation system (the “traffic source” behind most
  viral small-channel videos). It walks through two concrete actions the creator says drove one older video
  to keep pulling ~5,000 views every 48 hours:

1 Hack 1 — Optimize the video file name with a keyword before you upload.
2 Hack 2 — Turn on the “Featured video for returning subscribers” (Spotlight) channel setting so a
   chosen video auto-plays for visitors.


                  HACK 1 · DO THIS BEFORE YOU UPLOAD
          1       Optimize the video file name with a keyword                                       ■ 1:03–4:24


     YouTube reads the actual file name of the video you upload (not the title) to understand what the video
     is about and who to recommend it to. A generic export name (like CapCut’s default file name) gives the
     algorithm nothing to work with; a keyword-rich file name helps it start pushing the video right away.

     1.       Finish recording and editing your video (for example, in CapCut).
     2.       Go to YouTube and research a relevant keyword for your topic — the phrase your target viewer
              would actually search.
     3.       Save/export the finished video to your device.
     4.       Rename the file so it contains that keyword, replacing the default export name.
     5.       Upload the renamed file to YouTube as normal.

     ■ iPhone: when the export is done, rename the file directly in Files/Photos.
     ■ Android: save the file to Google Drive first, then rename it inside Drive.
     ■ Computer: send the video to your laptop, then rename it there before uploading.
     ■ Budget an extra 20–30 minutes for this — the creator credits it for up to ~10× more views.

                 HACK 2 · A CHANNEL SETTING TO TURN ON
         2       Turn on “Featured video for returning subscribers” (Spotlight)                                  ■ 4:25–7:46


    This channel setting makes a video you choose auto-play on your channel page for visitors, so it
    collects views even when no one clicks it. You set it from a phone by opening YouTube Studio in
    desktop mode.

    1.       Open Google Chrome on your phone and search “YouTube dashboard”; tap the result.
    2.       Tap Continue, then switch the browser to Desktop site — it must be the desktop version for this to
             work.
    3.       Click Customization.
    4.       Click the Home tab (you’ll see “Profile” and “Home tab”).
    5.       Toggle on to reveal the sections you can add to your home tab.
    6.       Add the section “Featured video for returning subscribers.”
    7.       Turn on “Spotlight for returning subscribers.”
    8.       When prompted, select the video you want to feature — pick one that genuinely offers value.
    9.       Optional: also set that same video as your Channel trailer so non-subscribers see it too.
    10. Click Save.

    ■ Result: returning subscribers (and, with the trailer, new visitors) land on your channel and the video starts
    playing — views without a click.



 Quick checklist

    ■ Researched a relevant keyword on YouTube for this video
    ■ Renamed the exported video file to include that keyword
    ■ Uploaded the renamed file
    ■ Opened YouTube Studio dashboard in Chrome, desktop mode
    ■ Customization → Home tab → added “Featured video for returning subscribers”
    ■ Turned on Spotlight and selected a high-value video
    ■ (Optional) Set the video as the channel trailer
    ■ Clicked Save



   One more tip from the video (8:19–end): keep views coming by optimizing the channel itself — use the right
   keywords in the right places (channel name, description, and tags) so YouTube can recommend both your
   videos and your channel.
