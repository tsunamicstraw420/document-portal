Overview

This lesson covers Kling o1 Edit (accessed on PromptEdit.com), a prompt-based video-editing model. The title's framing — "it's like Photoshop for video clips" — captures the idea: you take an existing video clip and make targeted edits with a text prompt (adding, removing, or changing elements within the clip) the way you'd use generative fill on a still image in Photoshop, except it works on moving footage while preserving the rest of the scene.

Where it fits

It's the last tool in the module's "edit existing footage" group, alongside Runway Aleph (VFX and new camera angles) and Wan 2.2 Replace (character and background replacement). Rather than generating a clip from scratch, these let you refine and transform footage you already have.
