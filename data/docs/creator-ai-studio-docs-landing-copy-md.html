FAQ

  • What's a credit? A unit you spend per generation. A thumbnail is ~1–9 credits; a short

video clip is more. The price is shown before you generate — nothing is a surprise.

  • Which AI models do I get? The current best across image and video — Flux, GPT Image,

Ideogram, Kling, Veo, and more — added as they ship. You never pick a provider; ShowKit routes to the right one.

  • Can I post what I make? Yes — everything you generate is made for publishing on your show

and socials. (Some models carry their own usage terms for edge cases; we surface those where they apply.)

  • Do unused credits expire? They roll over one billing cycle, so a light week isn't wasted.
  • Can I cancel? Anytime, in one click — your credits stay good through the period.
