Questions

Good to know

What's a credit?

A unit you spend per generation. A thumbnail is ~1–9 credits; a video clip is more. The price is shown before you generate — no surprises.

Which models do I get?

The current best across image and video — Flux, GPT Image, Ideogram, Kling, Veo, and more, added as they ship. You never pick a provider; ShowKit routes to the right one.

Can I post what I make?

Yes — everything is made for publishing on your show and socials. (Some models carry their own terms for edge cases; we surface those where they apply.)

Do unused credits expire?

They roll over one billing cycle, so a light week isn't wasted.
