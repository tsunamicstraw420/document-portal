Materially-off flags (>25% from live)

  1. GPT Image — the one that can go negative. $0.04 is right only for medium quality (live $0.042).

At high quality a 1024² costs ~$0.167 — 4× the assumption. At 8 credits ($0.16 list) a high-quality render sells below COGS. Even medium should be 9 credits (ceil(0.042/0.005)=8.4→9), not 8. This is the single biggest correction: it's the only model that can invert margin.

  1. Seedance — ~2× too high (safe direction). Assumed $0.50; live Seedance 1.5 Pro 720p ≈ $0.26/5s

(Lite ≈ $0.18). At 100 credits we'd overcharge the user (~$2 list for ~$0.26 COGS). Drop to 52 credits. Caveat: fal-ai/seedance is a generic route — Seedance 2.0 is far pricier (~$0.30/s ≈ $1.5/5s ≈ 300 cr). Pin the SKU before trusting either number.

  1. Ideogram — stale version + no matching SKU. ideogram-v2 is two generations behind; the live v3

line is Turbo $0.03 / Balanced $0.06 / Quality $0.09. $0.05 matches no current v3 SKU, and v2 standard is ~$0.08 (10 cr = only 60% list margin). Move to v3 Turbo $0.03 → 6 credits (matches the econ doc) or v3 Quality $0.09 → 18 credits.

Everything else verified within tolerance. SDXL is actually cheaper than assumed ($0.0026 vs $0.004) — conservative, still 1 credit. Note fal's Flux-1.1-Pro meta tag reads $0.055/MP while the on-page live cost calculator and Replicate both read $0.04 — treat $0.04 as current, re-verify at integration.

Verdict — does the ~75% gross thesis still hold?

Yes, with two line-item fixes. Seven of nine models priced on or below the assumed wholesale (Flux Schnell, Flux Dev, Flux 1.1 Pro, Kling, and Veo 3.1 match to the cent; SDXL and Seedance came in cheaper than assumed). Because the credit unit is derived from COGS and most COGS held flat or fell, the ceil(wholesale/$0.005) → sell-at-$0.02 structure still yields ~75% list gross across the catalog, and slightly better on the deflated items — exactly the reseller-benefits-from-deflation dynamic the econ doc predicts. The thesis's only real hole is GPT Image at high quality, where an unpinned quality tier turns an 8-credit charge into a loss; a distant second is Ideogram v2 standard at thinned margin. Pin GPT Image's quality (or add a high-quality SKU at ~34 credits) and repoint Ideogram to a live v3 SKU, and the 75%-list / mid-60s-to-70s realized-margin model is intact at today's prices.
