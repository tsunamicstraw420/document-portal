Music · tied to the joke

The epic score that shrinks

The interviews have no music at all, only room tone, like a real documentary. The montage gets a huge orchestral dinosaur theme with a tune you can hum. It cuts dead on "So how big was the crew?"

Then, during the pull-back, the same theme comes back, but small and tinny, as if it's playing from Dani's monitor speakers. The orchestra shrinks along with the picture. That tells you the whole blockbuster came out of that one desk. On the end card the theme opens back up to full size.

On-screen text · per the Motion Text Playbook

The crew list

The ad's idea is "one person is the whole crew". So the text is a film's crew credits, and the payoff is that every credit lands on one name.

  • Name captions in documentary style: a white bar wipes in, then the name types on. REX · LEAD ACTOR and REX JR. · STUNT DOUBLE.
  • Spoken lines rise in word by word in the empty part of the frame. The interviewer's lines type on in italic with a small "INTERVIEWER" tag, because he's never seen.
  • The film's title slams in at the start of the montage, with a roar hit.
  • The stunt gag: the lava leap freezes for a beat and an arrow snaps onto the jumper: ACTUALLY REX JR. (UNCREDITED). It pays off his line.
  • The crew roll during the pull-back, beside Dani. Each name spins like a slot machine and lands with a tick:
DIRECTORDANIWRITERDANICAMERADANIMAKEUPDANIMUSICDANIEDITORDANISTUNTSREX JR. ✓ finally
  • End card: the frame shrinks to a card, then BE THE WHOLE CREW. slams in with an impact hit, and the course lines rise in one by one.

The face detector can't see dinosaur faces, so I mark Rex's and Rex Jr.'s heads by hand on frame strips and keep every word off them.
