Hero picture ideas

The headline is "You just won a movie studio." The best picture says "movie studio" and makes a stranger smile in one glance.

New ideas

These don't exist yet. Each picture costs 17 credits to make, about $0.07 for you, and nothing has been made or spent.

My pick

1. Grandma directs the dragon

A tiny grandmother in a director's chair shouts through a megaphone at a full-size dragon on a film set. The crew scatters. She is not impressed. It says "movie studio" and "anything is possible" at once.

2. The dog runs the studio

A golden retriever in a beret sits in the director's chair, one paw on the camera, while a nervous human actor waits for notes.

3. The living room premiere

A family in pajamas on their own couch, with a red carpet, velvet ropes and flashing photographers packed around them. Their everyday life just became a movie event.

4. The gates open

Huge studio gates swing open at sunset, searchlights sweeping the sky, with "YOUR STUDIO" in lights overhead. It's the headline as a picture, the grandest and least funny of the five.

5. The crayon dragon escapes

A kid's crayon drawing of a dragon climbs off the paper on the kitchen table and becomes real, knocking over the cereal. It shows what Solorender does: imagination turns real.
