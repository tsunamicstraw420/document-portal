Good to know

Can I waste my credits? Not by accident. The button shows the price before you press it. On Auto it says “cost varies,” because Auto picks the engine when you press. If something fails, the credits come back on their own.

Where does my stuff go? Everything you make is saved in your Library, ready to download.

Does it work on my phone? Yes, for nearly everything. Tap the ☰ button at the top for the menu. Flow and the Video editor are better on a bigger screen.

Do my credits expire? No. Whatever you don't use stays in your account.

Is there a catch? None. There's no card on file and nothing renews, so you can't be charged by surprise. If you ever want more credits, you can buy them at the Studio rate, but you never have to.

Any rules? Two. You need to be 18 or over, and only use photos of people who are happy for you to use them.
