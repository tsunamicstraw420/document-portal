What each column means

  • Our recorded cost is what solorender worked out from Google's own usage numbers times Google's price list. It's what the 2.5× customer price is based on.
  • Google early estimate is Google's "Live costs" report. Google says these are rough, rounded to the cent, only cover some products, and may change.
  • Google real bill is the real charge, before the trial credit is taken off. Google says it can take more than 24 hours to show up.
  • The warning line is a Google charge more than 10% above ours. Only Gemini voice crossed it, and its price was already doubled to match.
