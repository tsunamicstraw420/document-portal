How to read it

  • Fit is my rough grade from their recent videos and description. Strong means two or more signs they cover the AI video tools Solorender sells (Kling, Veo, Seedance, Nano Banana, Higgsfield, AI ads and avatars, faceless channels), or they turned up in two or more searches. Some means one sign. Weak means AI or editing in general. Read their newest video before inviting.
  • Email: on YouTube rows it is copied from the creator's own channel description, so they chose to show it for business. On TikTok and Instagram rows it was found and checked as valid by Influencers.club (free trial credits). "None shown" means you'd reach them another way or skip them.
  • Followers and newest video for YouTube come from the official YouTube Data API, read November 2, 2026. TikTok and Instagram follower counts come from Influencers.club the same day; the last column shows their bio instead of a video.
  • The list leaves out channels that are AI companies or brands (Kling, CapCut, Higgsfield and so on), and channels mostly in another language. A few may still slip through.

Where creators could find the program on their own

Free reading only, done November 2, 2026. Nothing was signed up for. Every price below comes from the place's own page unless it says "not confirmed".

  • PromoteKit has no marketplace. Its own docs (promotekit.com/llms-full.txt) never mention a marketplace or directory. So no creator will find Solorender through PromoteKit itself.
  • AffyList (affylist.com): free. It's a directory of more than 500 programs, and creators can filter it by how they promote: YouTube, newsletter and so on. Its submit page offers two ways in. "Free with badge, $0" means its badge goes in our homepage footer, with review in 1 to 7 days. "Instant Featured, $5 once" means no badge. This is the only free directory confirmed so far. The badge would be a small change to the Solorender footer, so it needs your yes.
  • Reddit and Indie Hackers: free. The places are r/SideProject, r/affiliatemarketing, r/SaaS and an Indie Hackers post. Each subreddit has its own rules on self-promotion, and they haven't been read yet.
  • Only for companies on their own affiliate software: PartnerStack, Reditus, Dub, Referly and Affonso. Each lists a program only if that company runs on their software and pays for it. We'd have to leave PromoteKit.
  • Paid networks: Awin (first month free, then $49 a month in the US). Impact runs from $30 a month plus 2.5% of revenue, and its marketplace starts at $500 a month on a 12-month contract. Either one runs its own tracking alongside PromoteKit.
  • Paid directories, not confirmed: SaaS Affiliate is about $99 once, according to another site, not its own page. TapRefer is about $99 once and Affiliate Corner about $99 a year; both figures come from search snippets only. ClickBank charges $49.95 when a product is approved, but its page blocked us, and it's mostly for info products, so a poor fit.
  • Couldn't confirm: Rewardful, Tolt and FirstPromoter don't seem to run marketplaces (their pages returned "not found"). Beacons, Collabstr and Aspire don't show any way for an app like ours to list a program. Find Affiliates' page is a "coming soon" placeholder. ShareASale, Product Hunt, There's An AI For That and Futurepedia haven't been checked yet.
