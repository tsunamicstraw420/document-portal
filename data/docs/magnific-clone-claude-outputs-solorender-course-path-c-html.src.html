Course path C: Built, checked, ships at the next run

The AI Filmmaking course now shows students what their film costs. When they're short, it offers the right pack at the moment they need it. After lesson 25 it keeps them coming back with a monthly Series challenge, paid in credits.

In one minute
  • Film budget. Lesson "Checkpoint: your plan" now prices the whole film two ways from today's price list. The careful route costs about 1,258 credits and the heavy route about 4,678. One Studio month gives 4,450.

  • First challenge is November. The code ships tonight at 9 pm or tomorrow at 1 pm, a day before October ends. So November 1–30 is the first month, and anything entered earlier counts for November.

Part 2

The "top up" box, at the right moment

It appears at the top of a lesson only when the student's balance is below what that lesson's runs need on the careful route. For example, lesson 11 (the film clips) needs about 750 credits. The box names the one pack that covers the gap at the student's own plan rate, read from your live offers. If you change prices on /admin/offers, the box follows. Students with enough credits never see it. It also sits on the Series page, against the cost of one episode (about 461 credits).

Part 3

The monthly Series challenge

Built to your five answers from today:

  • Entries. A course student picks a finished video from their Library, names the series and the episode, and ticks a box agreeing it can be shown publicly. One entry per person per month. They can change it or take it back until the month closes.
  • Judging. You do it on the new Admin → Series challenge tab. "Approve" puts an entry on the public showcase. Once the month has closed, buttons for 1st, 2nd and 3rd appear. Your click pays the prize at once, using the same credit-granting step as your gifts and sponsor deals. The database refuses a second payout of the same place.
  • Prizes. 12,500 / 7,500 / 5,000 credits, shown to students as about 2.8, 1.7 and 1.1 Studio months. On the student's Credits page the row reads "Series challenge prize".
  • Showcase. solorender.com/series is public and in the sitemap. It shows approved episodes only, newest month first, with winners at the front. It has a search box and a sort menu, like the Prompt Library. Your owner page has them too.
  • Timing. Calendar months, Pacific time. Entries close at midnight on the last day, and you pick winners in the first week of the next month.

The course home also gets an "After the course" card leading to the challenge. Lesson 24 and the final checkpoint now link to it, and lesson 24's episode cost uses the same numbers as the challenge page.
