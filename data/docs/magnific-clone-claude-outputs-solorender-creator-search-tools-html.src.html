Alternatives to Heepsy and Modash

Tools for finding YouTube creators in AI, video and creative niches (mostly 5,000–250,000 subscribers), with real view counts and a business email, to invite into the partner program and for about 10 paid trial videos.

The answer

  • Don't buy anything yet. The free setup you already have finds most of what you need, and no paid tool proved it fills the one real gap.
  • The real gap is email. About 4 in 10 channels in these niches put a business email in their channel description, which the free YouTube API reads. The other 6 in 10 hide it behind YouTube's "View email address" button, which needs a CAPTCHA. No tool documented getting those, and YouTube's terms ban automated scraping.
  • Next step (free): take 20 channels with no email in their description and run them through the free trials of Modash, Clickanalytic, Influencers.club and Heepsy, plus ChannelCrawler's free preview. Count how many real emails each one adds. Only a tool that fills that gap is worth paying for. You'd start the trials yourself, since they need an account.
  • If none fills it: stay free, plus NanoInfluencer at $29 a month. It follows a creator's link-in-bio and website, where emails the description search misses often sit.

Three channels is a small sample, but it's a warning.

Every tool checked

What couldn't be verified

  • G2 and Capterra blocked direct reading, so their review scores come from search-result snippets. Trustpilot figures were read directly. Reddit was blocked, so no community threads were used.
  • No tool publishes how often it finds an email, or how many of its emails bounce. That's what the 20-channel trial test measures.
  • Conflicting or missing prices on vendors' own pages: Modash monthly, Influencers.club entry plan, Favikon labels, NanoInfluencer yearly, ChannelCrawler minimum export, trial length and terms (its terms page returned an error).
  • Audience-country filters for Clickanalytic, Heepsy and Influencers.club; terms for Favikon, Upfluence, Influencity, NanoInfluencer and TubeMailer; brand fees for Passionfroot, Agentio, Spotter and YouTube BrandConnect.
  • No independent evidence that any affiliate directory brings active affiliates. Database sizes are all the vendors' own claims. "Best tools" listicles were vendor marketing and weren't used as evidence.

Coverage: 28 tools in depth (own pricing page plus terms, independent reviews or a hands-on test), 39 only mentioned (blocked pages, enterprise quote-only, or the wrong kind of tool). Free reading only: no sign-ups, purchases or trial starts.
