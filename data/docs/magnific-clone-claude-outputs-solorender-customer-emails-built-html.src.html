Every email has your Concord address and an "Email settings" link that works without logging in. Mail apps also get a one-click unsubscribe. All 25 card links and pictures open on the live site. Preview every email anytime: Admin → Customer emails.

In the app: a low-credit bar at the top below 200 credits ("150 credits left · about 2 clips with sound"). Plan-holders see "Top up"; everyone else sees "See plans". It's hidden while automatic refill is on.

Credits & billing: an Automatic refill card. "Turn on" only works after ticking "I agree Solorender may charge my card $25 for each automatic refill, up to 2 a month, until I turn it off." It shows the customer's own pack size (2,750 credits on Studio monthly). There's also a "Tips and reminders" email switch.
