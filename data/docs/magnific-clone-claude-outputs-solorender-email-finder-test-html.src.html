Which tool finds the emails YouTube hides?

Below are 20 YouTube creators who fit solorender's partner program and show no email anywhere in their channel description. Look each one up in every free trial. A tool is worth paying for only if it finds real business emails for these people, because the emails it can find for everyone else, you already get for free.

Where things stand

  • Today's free YouTube search found 178 channels in the 5,000–250,000 subscriber range across the five niches. 79 of them (45%) already show an email in their description. The other 99 don't.
  • I picked these 20 from the 99: English-language, real creators (not brands or software companies), close to what solorender does, and every one posted a new video in the last two weeks.
  • Free no-account lookups, done October 29: Heepsy's free finder found 0 new emails for the 20. Modash's and Influencers.club's free finders wouldn't run in my browser. Details are in the next section.
  • Modash trial, checked November 2: 0 emails for the 7 channels checked (Jake Dawson, Alicia Lyttle, Mira AI, Mark Gadala-Maria, Gavin Herman, Caleb Boxx, Grace Leung). Modash only lists emails that are already public, so it misses the same hidden emails Heepsy missed. Its subscriber counts were current.

  • Nothing has been bought. You start each trial yourself; the steps are further down.

Start the free trials

You do the sign-ups. I never sign up, log in or type anything into your accounts. Use whatever email you want for these. Each step was checked against the tool's own page on October 29, 2026.

The same routine in every tool

  1. Search each of the 20 channels by name or handle (the Copy buttons in the table above help).
  2. Before unlocking anything, check whether the tool marks an email as available. If it does, spend your free unlocks only on those channels. The number marked is a useful result in itself.
  3. Note the subscriber count the tool shows for 3 or 4 channels so I can compare it with today's live count.
  4. When you're done, leave the results tab open in Chrome and tell me. I'll read it (view only, no clicks) and fill in the scorecard. Or paste what you saw into chat.
Account trial · free

Modash

14 days, no card. Up to 20 profiles viewed and 6 email unlocks. Paid plan afterwards is $199 a month, but the page doesn't say the trial turns into it on its own.
  1. Open marketer.modash.io/register/marketer and create the account.
  2. Choose YouTube, then search a channel by name, or paste its YouTube link.
  3. Open the profile and look for the email or an unlock button.
With only 6 unlocks, use them on the channels where it shows an email exists.
Account trial · free

Clickanalytic

14 days, no card. 10 email unlocks, 10 creator analyses, 100 searches a day. Its page says it unlocks "the emails creators list publicly", so it may only repeat what's visible.
  1. Open app.clickanalytic.com/register and create the account.
  2. Search the channel by name in its YouTube search.
  3. Unlock the email on channels that show one.
Its terms (section 9.12) limit collecting personal data about others. Read only for this test; ask them in writing before any paid use.
Account trial · free

Influencers.club

10 free credits plus Pro features. "No payment details are required to start." It claims verified emails, "weekly updated".
  1. Open dashboard.influencers.club/register and create the account.
  2. Look each channel up by username or channel ID. The channel ID is the code after /channel/ in each table link.
  3. Spend the 10 credits on the channels that show an email.
Account · free search

ChannelCrawler

Free search, "No credit card required". Each email costs one email credit ("View Email" button). Paid plans have a 7-day trial; I couldn't confirm whether that needs a card.
  1. Open app.channelcrawler.com/auth/register and create a free account.
  2. Search a channel name as the keyword.
  3. Count which of the 20 show a "View Email" button. Don't start the paid trial without telling me first.
It searches by filters (views, subscribers, keyword), not by one channel's link, so some of the 20 may be hard to find.
