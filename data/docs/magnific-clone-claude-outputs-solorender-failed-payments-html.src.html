When a customer's card fails, we now try to keep them

Failed card payments cause 24–38% of lost subscribers, and about half of them can be won back. Until today the site ignored a failed payment completely. Now Stripe warns the customer, keeps trying their card, and the app shows them how to fix it.

Stripe settings: live now Site change: saved, goes live at the next 1 pm or 9 pm update Not yet seen with a real customer
What a customer sees now

If their monthly or yearly renewal doesn't go through

  • Stripe emails them straight away. The email's button opens Stripe's secure page where they type a new card. No Solorender login is needed. Before today, no email was sent.
  • Stripe tries the card again, 8 times over 2 weeks. Stripe picks the times most likely to work. Before, it was 4 tries over 3 weeks.
  • A notice appears at the top of every Solorender page, with an Update card button that opens the card form directly:
    Your last payment didn't go through. Update your card to keep your plan.
  • Their plan keeps working meanwhile (your choice): jobs at once, the course, top-up packs, Connect Claude, the Prompt Library. The month's new credits arrive only once a payment succeeds.
  • If all 8 tries fail, the plan ends on its own. The credits they already have stay, as always.
  • The Help page "If a payment fails" now explains all of this.
What it costs

Nothing extra. Stripe's price page lists the retries and the reminder emails as "Included" in the Stripe Billing fee you already pay (0.7% of billing). No new supplier, no new tool.

Still open

Not yet seen with a real failed payment. There are no paying customers yet, so the first real case will be the true test. I also haven't seen Stripe's email itself, because Stripe doesn't send emails in test mode.

Automatic card updates are unknown. This is when a bank tells Stripe about a customer's new card number by itself. There is no switch for it in your Stripe dashboard. Stripe's own help page doesn't say whether it's on for you, or what it costs.

A separate billing bug, found by the other session today. When someone books a switch from monthly to yearly, Stripe never charges them for the year. Its fix needs a change in the same payment code I worked in. I didn't make that change, because it wasn't part of this job. That code is now free for the other session to finish.

An option for later: Stripe can also email customers before their card expires, which stops some failures before they happen. It's free and currently off. I left it off because you didn't ask for other emails.
