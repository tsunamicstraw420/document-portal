1What Solorender is

On September 10, 2026, the first version of Solorender went live at solorender.com: one studio page, a simple canvas and a library. Eight days later it could take real card payments. I built it alone, directing AI coding tools that write the code, and everything since has been built in the open, on the live site: about 11 of every 12 lines of today's code were written after that first version went live.3

Creators and small businesses today juggle several AI subscriptions: one app for pictures, another for video, another for voices, another for music, another for editing. Each has its own monthly plan, and most of them wipe unused credits at the end of the month.

Solorender replaces that pile with one studio and one wallet. Customers buy credits and use them on any model I carry: Google's Veo and Nano Banana, OpenAI's GPT Image, Kling, Seedance, ElevenLabs, FLUX and more. Their credits never expire, and the $8 starter offer and the bulk Super Max pack need no subscription at all.

The prices. One credit is worth 1 cent at retail. The $8 starter offer buys 1,100 credits once. The Solo plan is $20 a month for 2,000 credits; Studio is $40 for 4,450; Max is $80 for 9,450. Super Max is a one-time $300 pack of 42,750 credits. The bigger plans include bonus credits, and Studio, Max and Super Max include the AI Filmmaking course. Yearly plans cost about 15% less.3

How that compares. Of 10 image and video studios I checked on their own pages on October 28, 7 let unused plan credits expire: OpenArt, Higgsfield, Magnific, VEED, Artlist, Midjourney and Sunra. The 3 that carry credits over put a limit on it: Leonardo keeps up to 3 months, Runway keeps 1 month on its $95 plan, and Krea only on business plans. None keeps plan credits forever, as Solorender does.26

On October 20 I cut the menu from 112 models to 56, keeping a top choice, a value choice and a cheap draft for each kind of job, so a newcomer isn't facing a wall of names.

For at least the next 60 days, the AI Filmmaking course is the main way I'll bring in customers. Affiliates and content creators on YouTube and X come first and lead with it; ads come third and lead with it too.

25 lessons in 5 modules: plan your film, make your pictures, shoot your clips, edit and finish, publish and grow. Every lesson ends with something the student has made. One running example, Agnes, an 84-year-old who secretly wins her town's downhill soapbox derby, is built step by step inside Solorender.

The course comes with the Studio plan ($40 a month), Max and Super Max. Solo customers see the full outline and an upgrade button. So a customer who comes for the course is worth about twice a Solo customer.

3Proof the category works

Solorender is a multi-model studio: it resells the best models from many AI labs in one app instead of training its own. Every figure below appears in at least two published sources, though most are figures the companies reported about themselves.

  • Magnific (formerly Freepik) uses, in its own words, "the same frontier models as our competitors." It reports $230 million in yearly recurring revenue and more than 1 million paying subscribers, with no venture capital.37

4What I have built

Pictures

Create images from a sentence, then edit them by describing the change. Erase objects, enlarge and sharpen, grow a square into a banner, and keep the same person across new scenes. 22 picture models and editors, from one-credit drafts to studio-grade.

Video

Turn a picture into a moving clip, or go straight from a written scene in wide, tall or square. Clips up to 30 seconds, many with sound built in, and several camera shots in a single clip.

Clip tools

Add seconds, upscale to 4K, rewrite a scene by typing, change a clip's shape while the AI fills the new space, give a silent clip its sound, or grab any frame to start the next shot. Act It Out: film yourself acting, and a character copies your moves.

Voice, music & sound

Professional voiceovers in 29 languages, full songs with vocals, background music and sound effects from a description, all from the same wallet.

Talking photos

Pick a face and a voice and the person speaks, with matching lip movement. Customers can also re-sync any clip to a new voice track, including a recording of their own voice.

Video editor

My own timeline editor, open to customers on any plan since October 22: trim, titles, voice and music, captions written automatically from the speech, and free export.

Make vertical

One click turns a wide clip into a tall 9:16 copy for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, following the faces. It runs in the customer's browser, so it uses no credits and costs me nothing. Studio plan and up.

Flow & films

A visual canvas for whole projects. One button, Render film, stitches clips, voice, captions and sound into a finished video, with the sound levelled the way a broadcast editor would.

Recipes

54 ready-made productions: a narrated product ad from one photo, a creator testimonial, a two-host podcast clip, a shop listing set, a headshot pack and more, in Saver, Standard and Best price levels. A recipe that kept failing my checks was dropped.

Characters & products

Save a person, character or product once, then call it by name in any prompt. The video models keep that face or product the same from shot to shot, the hardest problem in AI video and the one brands care about most.

Prompt Library & styles

173 published ready-to-use prompts in 17 categories (145 more in testing), each with a public page Google can find, and 198 original visual styles customers can apply or make from their own pictures.

Direct button & AI agents

One click turns a rough idea into a full prompt, including how characters act and what to avoid. Customers can also run Solorender from inside Claude and other AI assistants.

Learning

A 25-lesson AI filmmaking course for the larger plans, 24 public model and tool guides with real results, a "How to prompt" guide with videos, a help center of about 50 pages, and the Solorender YouTube channel.

Plans that scale

Bigger plans run more jobs at once, jump the queue for films and get more storage, from 5 GB up to 2 TB. The product itself enforces these limits.

6Affiliates: customers with no upfront cost

Affiliates are my first channel, and they have been the plan from the start. An ad has to be paid for before anyone buys. An affiliate customer costs me nothing until they pay, and the affiliate's share comes out of that payment.

Two words used in this letter: affiliates are anyone who signs up for a personal link and earns a share of what their customers pay. Content creators are a smaller group of AI creators on YouTube and X, people who post how-tos and workflows, whom I pay to show Solorender in a video or a thread and give a free Studio plan, to find the few who sell. The ones who sell keep earning as affiliates.

  • How it works. Anyone can join at solorender.com/affiliates and get a personal link. A visitor who clicks it has 60 days to make a first purchase; the affiliate then earns 25% of the plans, Super Max packs and $8 starter offers that customer buys for 12 months. Top-up packs earn nothing.
  • Paid only from money already in. Commissions are paid 30 days after each month ends, once an affiliate reaches $50. A refund reverses the commission automatically, which I tested with a real purchase and refund on October 10.
  • Affiliates who bring in other affiliates can earn 5% of what their recruits bring in, from my share; the recruit still gets the full 25%.
  • Live and ready. Tracking has run since October 9, and I've listed 303 creators, newsletters and communities to invite, with invitations written.
  • How the terms compare. Higgsfield pays "up to 25%" for up to 12 months, Arcads 25% for 12 months, Synthesia 25%, Hedra 15% for a year, and Magnific 10% of the first payment only. Runway pays $15 per new subscriber and gives partners a free Max plan.25 Software affiliate programs typically pay 20–25%.23, 24

9Risks and how I reduce them

These are the risks I would ask about in your place.

One founder

Everything here was built and is run by one person. The site runs on automated checks (a watchdog every 30 minutes, and checks every 5 minutes that message my phone only when something breaks), and the raise funds 18 months of full-time founder pay.

A short sales history

Solorender is newly launched, so there are only weeks of sales to judge by. The launch leads with affiliates, who are paid only from sales, content creators on YouTube and X, and an $8 no-subscription starter offer. Ads get only a small test at first.

Weak payback on published averages

On the cheapest plan, an ad-won customer pays back within a year only in the best case (section 5). That is why affiliates come first and ads scale only if Solorender's own test beats that. Yearly plans, bigger plans and unused credits all improve it.

Rivals sell below my cost

Some competitors price below what suppliers charge me. I lead with what they don't offer: credits that never expire and no-subscription buying. The Runware deposit and buying direct from Google lower my prices further.

Dependence on AI suppliers

I buy from three suppliers (Google, fal.ai and Runware) and can move a model between them. I read each supplier's terms before reselling a model, and keep a model hidden until its terms allow resale.

A fast-moving market

Models improve every few weeks. Because I resell rather than train, I can add a new model within days and drop one that falls behind, as the October menu cut did.

12Terms

Up to $200,000 on Y Combinator's standard post-money SAFE with a $5,000,000 valuation cap and no discount. At the cap, the full $200,000 converts into 4.0% of the company.57

In plain terms: a SAFE is the standard agreement in which your money turns into shares when the company later raises a priced funding round. The $5 million cap is the highest company value used for that conversion, so if a later round values Solorender higher, your money still converts at $5 million. "Post-money" means the 4.0% already counts this money. "No discount" means there is no extra price cut on top of the cap. Y Combinator, the startup program, publishes this agreement free for anyone to use; using it unchanged means there is nothing unusual to review.57

For context: Carta's median cap for SAFE rounds under $250,000 was $7.5 million in mid-2025 and $8 million in mid-2026. Most SAFEs carry a cap and no discount.56
