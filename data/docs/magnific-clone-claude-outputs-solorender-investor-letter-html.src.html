What solorender is

Creators and small businesses today juggle five or six AI subscriptions. One app for pictures, another for video, another for voices, another for music. Each has its own monthly plan, and most of them wipe your unused credits at the end of the month.

solorender replaces that pile with one studio and one wallet. Customers buy credits and use them on any model we carry: Google's Veo and Nano Banana, OpenAI's GPT Image, Kling, Seedance, ElevenLabs, FLUX and many more. Their credits never expire.

We checked this against five competitors (OpenArt, Magnific, Higgsfield, VEED and Artlist). None of them roll unused credits over. That is the promise we lead with.

What we have built

The product, piece by piece

Pictures

Create images from a sentence, then edit them by describing the change. Erase objects, enlarge and sharpen, grow a square into a banner, and keep the same person across new scenes. 45 picture models and editors, from one-credit drafts to studio-grade.

Video

Turn a picture into a moving clip, or go straight from a written scene. Clips up to 30 seconds, up to 4K, many with sound built in, and several camera shots in a single clip. Then keep working on them: add seconds, upscale, restyle a whole clip into animation, or change it by typing ("make it winter").

Voice & music

Studio voiceovers in 29 languages, full songs with vocals, and background music, all from the same wallet.

Talking photos

Pick a face and a voice and the person speaks, with matching lip movement. Customers can also upload their own clip and re-sync it to any voice track.

Flow & films

A visual canvas where every step of a project sits side by side. One button, Render film, stitches the clips, voice, captions and sound into a single finished video. We run our own film-assembly server for this, and it evens out the sound levels the way a broadcast editor would.

Recipes

54 ready-made productions. Drop in one product photo and get a three-shot narrated ad, a creator-style testimonial, a two-host podcast clip, a six-picture shop listing set, a professional headshot pack, a mock courtroom drama about your product, and many more. Video recipes come in three price levels (Saver, Standard and Best), so a customer can draft cheaply and finish in top quality.

Every film recipe is checked the way a customer sees it: the whole finished film, start to end. Recipes that don't pass are not listed.

Prompt Library

318 ready-to-use prompt ideas in 16 categories, each one click away from running. 58 of them come with a real preview clip. It is a perk for paying customers.

Characters & products

Save a person, character or product once, then mention it by name in any prompt. The video models keep that face or product looking the same from shot to shot. This is the hardest problem in AI video, and it is the one brands care about most.

Styles

A library of 197 original visual looks (anime, clay, noir comics, storybook watercolor, pixel art and many more), each with our own example pictures. Customers can also make a style from their own pictures and apply it to images, recipes and whole clips.

Direct button

Not everyone knows how to write a prompt. One click, and our built-in AI director writes it for them, including what the model should avoid.

AI-agent access

Customers can run solorender from inside Claude and other AI assistants. The assistant can generate, build a project and render the film on its own.

Learning

A help center with 47 articles, model-by-model guides, an AI filmmaking course for customers on our larger plans, and a public home page full of example clips and pictures.

Plans that scale

Bigger plans run more jobs at the same time, jump the queue for films, and get more storage, from 5 GB up to 2 TB. The product itself enforces these limits.

The business underneath

A creative tool is only a company if the money works. We built that side with the same care.

  • Live payments since September 18. Monthly and yearly plans, an $8 starter offer for new customers, a one-time bulk credit pack, and top-up packs for plan holders. All run through Stripe and were tested with real money end to end.
  • A simple, fair margin. Every generation is priced at 2.5 times what it costs us. When our cost drops, the customer's price drops with it. We never quietly keep the difference.
  • Cost already going down. On October 14 we brought on a second AI supplier, Runware. A five-second clip from Seedance 2.0, one of the leading premium video models, now costs us $0.79 instead of $1.52. If that supplier has a problem, the job is refunded and the customer can try again.
  • Supplier sales passed on automatically. When our main supplier puts a model on sale, our price follows within hours. A check runs three times a day without anyone touching it.
  • Built-in growth channel. A creator partner program is live. Partners earn 25% of the plans and offers their referrals buy, for 12 months, tracked automatically.
  • Protected against losses. Refunds and card disputes take the credits back. Failed jobs refund the customer automatically. Video is billed on the real length of the file, not on what the browser claims.
  • Prices editable without code. The owner console lets us change plans, offers and packs, give or take credits, pause a model, and see real money in against real supplier cost.

Built to run without babysitting

  • A watchdog checks the live site every 30 minutes around the clock.
  • Every 5 minutes, the system checks for stuck films, supplier refusals and a silent film server, and sends an alert to my phone.
  • Long video jobs finish and save to the customer's library even if they close the browser.
  • Every customer file is kept in our own low-cost cloud storage, so we don't depend on a supplier's short-lived links.
  • We found and fixed a problem early where a slow job could be charged twice. It cannot happen now.

One founder. No outside money. A full studio, live payments and a company behind it in about two months.
