First 50 Outreach Emails

Short, friendly notes from you, each opening with something real of theirs, signed with a Solorender-branded signature. They go to the 50 strongest prospects with an email address: 43 best-fit and 7 good-fit.

ShortAbout 90–110 words. The best first emails run 50–100, and longer ones get fewer replies.
PersonalEach opens with their own video, course or posts, plus a subject line with their name. Personal subject lines roughly double replies.
Branded, lightlyYour colors live in the signature: logo badge, green rule and one green button. Heavy designs get sorted into Gmail's Promotions tab.

Send yourself a test first

Click Copy email on #1, open Gmail, address it to your own email, paste and send. Check it on your phone and computer before sending real ones.

How to send each one (in Chrome, signed in to support@solorender.com)

  1. Copy email: copies the message with its branded signature.
  2. Open in Gmail: opens a new email from support@ with the address and subject filled in.
  3. Click into the message area and paste (Cmd+V). Check the From line, then press Send.
  4. Tick Sent. About 10 a day for the first week keeps your new address in good standing.
