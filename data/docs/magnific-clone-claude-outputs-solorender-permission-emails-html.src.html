Supplier Permission Emails

Nine short emails that ask a supplier for a written "yes, your use is allowed." Each yes unlocks a cheaper source. Our price is always real cost × 2.5, so every saving goes straight to customers. A tenth email, to Runware, is parked at the bottom, as you asked.

Nothing has been sent. No form was filled and no account was made. You send each one yourself from eddie@solorender.com. Press Copy on To, Subject and Body, then paste them into your email.

Before you send

  • Orange blanks like [EDDIE: …] are for numbers only you can give, such as how much you expect to use. I left them blank on purpose rather than invent anything. Fill them in, or delete the line.
  • Every email says the site is live but in final testing, with the affiliate rollout coming shortly. None says "launched in September."
  • Every email mentions the MCP connector honestly, because several of these terms talk about "API access" or "integrating into third-party apps."
  • Three clauses read differently from our notes, so I quote what's live today: Atlas, Topaz and Segmind. Each card says what changed.
  • Atlas's Veo saving is small next to a free step. Google direct already gives Veo 3.1 Fast at $0.80 a clip with no permission needed. Atlas would be $0.768. Atlas is still worth asking for its Kling and Wan prices.
  • Topaz may not save anything. At 1080p, Runware already sells it below Topaz's own price. The 20% saving is a 4K figure from the research page, and I didn't re-check 4K prices today.
