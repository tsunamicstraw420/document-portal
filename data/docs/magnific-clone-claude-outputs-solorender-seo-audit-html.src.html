The three things that matter most

  1. Fix www.solorender.com. It shows a security warning today.

    The site's security certificate covers only solorender.com. Anyone who types www.solorender.com gets a "your connection is not private" page instead of the site. Google also keeps an old www copy as a duplicate. The fix is adding www to the domain on Vercel and pointing it at the main address. It takes about 10 minutes.

  2. Tell Google the site exists, then open up what's locked.

    Your 7 Learn articles are in the sitemap, but Google hasn't indexed any of them. Google showed a "Try Search Console" prompt, so the site is probably not registered there yet. Register it, submit the sitemap, then make public, read-only versions of Recipes (47), Styles (197) and the help center (37 pages). Visitors can browse, and the "Use this" button asks them to sign in.

  3. Build the pages your rivals win with.

    Higgsfield, OpenArt and Krea all use the same pattern: a page per model ("Kling 3.0", "Seedance 2.0") with prices and FAQs, "AI ___ generator" use-case pages, "X vs Y" comparisons and "alternative to X" pages. The "alternative" and "credits never expire" searches are won today by tiny startups. A new site like yours can win them too.
