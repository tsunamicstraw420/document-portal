3. What to improve

Biggest

Style pages are too thin

214 of our 359 pages are style pages, and each one carries only about 60 words of its own. That's one picture, two sentences, three "good for" tags and a how-to line. The rest is the menu and footer every page shares. Google often reads pages like this and then leaves them out ("Crawled – not indexed"). A pile of thin pages can also make Google rate the whole site lower.

Fix: give each style page real substance. Show several real pictures in that style on different subjects, which models it works best on, and a sample prompt. The catch is that we only have a cover picture for most styles. More examples would mean either using results customers already made with the style (if any exist; I haven't checked) or a paid batch run, which needs your OK. A free stop-gap: merge the 16 family pages into stronger hub pages, and link each style to the matching Tools and Recipes pages.

Big

Almost no one links to us yet

For a new site, links from other sites are Google's main reason to trust it and read it faster. Free routes: listings in AI tool directories (many take free submissions; check each one's rules), a Product Hunt launch, our affiliates linking from their own sites and videos, and your social profiles pointing to the Learn tests. The side-by-side tests are the most shareable pages we have.

Medium

No "labels" for Google on 358 pages

Only the homepage carries the hidden labels that tell Google what a page is. Adding them is free code work:

  • Video labels on Learn pages. This would let our real clips show in Google's video results. Right now Search Console says "No video indexed".
  • Article labels on guides and tests, with the date updated.
  • Breadcrumbs (Home › Styles › Cartoon › Acid Pop) on every page. Google shows these in place of the address.
Medium

The sitemap has no "last updated" dates

Our list gives Google 359 addresses but never says when a page changed. With real dates, Google knows which pages to re-read first, for example after a Learn guide gets new results.

Medium

Every page opens with the same ad bar

The first text Google reads on every page is the announcement bar ("New: Nano Banana 2.1 · Try it…"), repeated several times in the page code. That pushes each page's own words down and makes 359 pages start identically. Fix: keep the bar for visitors but have Google skip it.

Small

Tidy-ups

  • 8 Learn titles are longer than Google shows (over 65 characters), so they get cut off with "…".
  • 9 recipe pages are on the thin side (under 250 words including the menu).
  • Terms, privacy, support and a few help pages have very short descriptions, under 70 characters.
  • Style pages link only to other styles. They should also point to the Tools, Recipes and Learn pages that use them, so Google finds them by more than one road.
  • Page speed scores (Core Web Vitals): Google shows "No data" yet. It needs more visitors first. Check again in a month.
