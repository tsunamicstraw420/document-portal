1

Grab a frame

Build now · free

Filmmakers chain clips by starting clip B from the last picture of clip A. Our own AI Filmmaking course tells customers "solorender doesn't have a button for this", so today they have to screenshot it themselves.

What the customer does

On any finished clip they click 📸 Last frame, or pause anywhere and click 📸 This frame. The picture lands in their Library and can go straight in as the start picture of the next clip.

Where it lives

On clip results in Studio, in the Library viewer, and on video boxes in Flow.

Cost

No supplier is involved. The customer's own browser takes the picture from the clip. We store it like any uploaded picture. 0 credits, $0 per use.

Checked today

All 465 saved clips are in our own storage. That storage already lets solorender.com read video frames, which is the one thing that could have blocked this.

Your call

Recommend: build it now, on all three places (Studio, Library, Flow), with both buttons.

Reply "build frame grab", or name the places you want it.

2

Sound effects

High value · small build

Many of our video models make silent clips, and so do customer uploads. Two jobs are needed:

  • Typed effect: type "heavy wooden door creaks open" and get a sound file.
  • Sound for a clip: pick a silent clip and get it back with matching footsteps, wind and impacts, timed to what's on screen.

None of our models does either today. The only CassetteAI model we sell makes music.

Medium value · medium build

Take a person out of a clip and put them in front of a new picture or clip.

Kling O3 4K Edit can already do it from a description, but it costs 525 credits for 5 s. It also redraws the whole clip, so the person can change slightly.

5

Your voice or a video as a reference

Medium value · medium build
Already works today (checked live)

A voice a customer records and uploads in the Video editor already shows up in Talk and Lip Sync under "Pick from Library". I opened that picker on solorender.com in your Chrome and your uploaded "welcome-voice" was in the list. So "make my character say my own recorded words" is already possible. The one limit: uploading a voice is only in the Video editor, which is for paid plans.

The new part is different. Some newer clip models take a voice file or a sample video alongside the picture and prompt, and use it to shape the clip:

  • A reference video lets the new clip copy its camera move, timing or style.
  • A reference voice shapes the sound of the clip.

Seedance 2.0 is the leading one, and we already run Seedance 2.0 on Runware. So this means adding upload slots to a model we already sell, not adding a new model.
