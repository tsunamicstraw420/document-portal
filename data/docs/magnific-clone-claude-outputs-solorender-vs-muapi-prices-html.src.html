muapi.ai vs Solorender: every picture and video model

I read muapi's public pricing page (no login). It holds all 703 of their models; 485 of them make pictures or video. Below, each of our 49 visible picture and video models sits next to muapi's price for the same job, in dollars, for the same clip length or picture.

The short version

37of our 49 models have a muapi price I could line up exactly
32of those, we charge more than muapi (at $0.01 a credit)
2we charge less
10same models where muapi sells for less than it costs US to buy
  1. Video is where the gap is. Veo 3.1 Fast: we charge $2.00 for an 8 s clip, muapi $0.60. Seedance 2.5: $2.88 vs $1.70. Kling v3 Pro: $2.10 vs $0.72.
  2. On some models muapi's selling price is under our buying price. Veo 3.1 Fast and Lite, Kling v3 Pro, Nano Banana 2 (and its Edit), Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2 Edit. Even at zero markup we couldn't match those.
  3. We win on a few pictures: Ideogram V4 ($0.03 vs $0.06), FLUX Klein ($0.01 vs $0.0104). Grok Imagine, Ideogram Character and Crisp Upscale are the same price.
  4. muapi doesn't sell Recraft, Bria Expand, VEED Fabric, DreamActor, H3 Reframe, Kling O3 4K Edit or Kling Lip Sync.

"Our price" = credits × $0.01 (Solo monthly $20 for 2,000 credits, and credit packs). On Max yearly ($68 for 9,450) a credit is $0.0072, shown in its own column. "Our cost" is what we pay our supplier, from the note beside each model in our code. muapi prices are taken as written on their page; where they say only "per generation", I note which clip length I assumed.
