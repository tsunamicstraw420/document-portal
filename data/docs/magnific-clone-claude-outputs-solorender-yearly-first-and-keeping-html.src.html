2. "Switch to a smaller plan" before cancelling

What customers see

On Credits & billing, under Manage plan, there is a new Cancel plan link. It opens "Before you cancel", which offers each smaller plan on the same billing period. Example: Studio monthly can pick "Switch to Solo — 2,000 credits a month, $20 a month".

The switch starts on their renewal date. Nothing is charged today, and they keep every credit. Below it, "No thanks — cancel my plan" opens Stripe's page, where they cancel as before. Solo customers (already the smallest plan) see a reminder that their credits stay, then the cancel button. The help article now describes this step.

Why I didn't put it inside Stripe's billing page

The brief said to switch on Stripe's own plan-change feature. I read Stripe's docs page first. Doing it there would have needed new Stripe products and a change to how credits are handed out, so I built it on our own page instead. Here's the trade-off:

Inside Stripe's pageOur own step (built)
New Stripe productsYes. Stripe needs a fixed product for every plan and price. Today each checkout makes its price on the spot.None
When you change a price on Prices & offersNew Stripe prices must be made and the old ones retired, every timeNothing extra
Downgrade at end of periodStripe only waits for the renewal when both prices belong to one productYes, already works
Right credits after the switchNo. Stripe doesn't update the plan details our credit system reads, so the customer would keep getting the old plan's credits.Yes. Proven in Stripe test mode: Studio monthly → Solo monthly bills $20 at renewal and grants 2,000 credits.
Cost to run$0, but more moving parts$0
Downside—Someone who opens Manage plan and cancels directly inside Stripe skips the offer

If you'd still like the offer inside Stripe's page as well, it is buildable. It needs the new Stripe products and the credit-system change above.

4. A money bug I found while testing

I tested every kind of booked plan switch in Stripe's free test mode, using a fake clock jumped past the renewal date:

Customer books…What Stripe does at renewalResult
Studio monthly → Solo monthlyBills $20, gives the Solo creditsCorrect
Studio yearly → Solo monthlyBills $20, gives the Solo creditsCorrect
Studio monthly → Studio yearlyNo bill at all. Their paid month is stretched into a full year at the yearly price. No credits either.Wrong: we'd lose $408, and they'd get no credits
Solo monthly → Studio yearlySame: no billWrong

This was in the plan switcher built on 14 Oct, not in today's work. Nobody has hit it, because there are no real customers yet. Now that we push yearly, it matters.

Fixed (commit cd1eb01). The fix has two halves, and both went in together. First, the switch now starts a fresh billing date, so Stripe bills the $408 at renewal. Second, the code that hands out credits now accepts that bill, because Stripe labels it a "plan change" rather than a "renewal". It still skips an upgrade's part-month bill, because upgrades already give their own credits.
