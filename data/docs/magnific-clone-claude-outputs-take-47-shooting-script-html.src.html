Words on screen

The idea: the clapperboard keeps score. The take number is the running joke, climbing from 1 to 47 while Dottie's patience runs out. The verdict lands like a rubber stamp: a red CUT! after each disaster and a green PRINT IT. at the end.

  • Slam in, with a hit: every clapperboard and stamp arrives with a shake and a matching sound.
  • Never on a face: faces are measured frame by frame, and the dragon's and cat's are marked by hand because the detector only finds people.
  • The quiet moments stay empty: no words during the finger wag or the twist.
  • Made in the edit: Remotion, using the same parts as your "Same Face" ad. Twelve test stills come first, before any full render.

Music and sound

Music: one hummable comedy-epic theme, the style that won on The Pitch. It's built for this film's joke: a huge orchestra that collapses into tinny laptop speakers at the twist. I'd make three options for you to choose from.

Dottie's voice: an older woman's voice that sounds sweet and dangerous. You'll hear each line before it goes in, because voices get checked by ear.

Sound effects: crashes and whooshes come from your own sound library and the clips themselves. Three special ones get made: the dragon sneeze, the wet sandwich squish and the tiny cat sneeze.
