How "The Pitch" was built in Flow

This is a click-by-click video for the course. Ruby's voice walks the customer through building the ad on a Flow canvas. A short, optional Video editor finish comes at the end. It is about 4 minutes, in 16:9.

How it gets made. This copies the method behind the "How to prompt" videos.

  • The recorder drives the real app on this Mac. It uses your real Flow canvas 257 and the real pictures, voices and clips from it.
  • Generate is pressed on screen. The press never reaches a model: it is answered with the result we already made. No credits are spent on screen.
  • Ruby's voice is laid over the screen, with the zooms onto each click. Ruby is not on camera.
  • The ad's own score plays underneath. It eases down smoothly whenever Ruby talks.

The segments

0:00–0:15

1 · Open on the result

About 10 seconds of the finished ad: the trailer, the cigar dropping, then "Sign him." After that comes the title card: Build The Pitch in Flow.

FREEIt uses the approved 16:9 ad.

0:15–0:35

2 · The map: one canvas, two rows

This is canvas 257 exactly as you built it. The pointer moves along each row:

  • The top row is the boardroom: the pictures, the voices and the talking shots.
  • The bottom row is Milo's trailer: the hamster heist.

The message is that every box is one run, and a wire carries each result to the next box.

0:35–1:40

3 · Build a talking shot, live

  1. Add a Picture box and pick Mr. Vance from the Library.
  2. Add a Voice box and type "A hamster heist?!".
  3. Pick the voice Algenib from the voice list.
  4. Type the "How it's said" direction: Booming, gravelly old Hollywood studio boss…. Ruby reads the whole direction aloud while it is typed.
  5. Press Generate, and the line plays.
  6. Add a Talk box and drag the two wires: picture to face, voice to voice.
  7. Press Generate, and Vance shouts.
1:40–2:45

4 · Fix the picture before you animate it

The laser picture is already on the canvas. Ruby points out the problem with the first try: a beam runs through Hammond's head.

  1. Add an Edit box and type the fix: "Move the red laser beams so that NONE of them touches the hamster…".
  2. Generate, and now the beams miss him.
  3. Add a Video box and pick Grok Video 1.5 Lite from the model list. Type the motion prompt.
  4. Generate, and he creeps under the lasers.

The lesson: fixing a picture is cheap, and redoing a clip is not.

2:45–3:10

5 · The rest of the heist, quick tour

Nothing is typed here. The pointer visits the finished boxes:

  • The vault: one picture turned into one clip.
  • The motorbike: the clip starts from a picture with both wheels on the desk. A mid-jump picture just hangs in the air.
  • The cigar: a close-up made from Vance's own picture.

Ruby also says that the bike clip was made longer with Extend in Clip tools.

3:10–3:55

6 · Optional: finish in the Video editor

Ruby says first: "This part is optional. Every clip is already in your Library, so you can finish wherever you like."

  1. Press 🎞 Open in video editor. The clips land in order.
  2. Add the score from the Library onto the Music row. Trim it to the film and turn it down.
  3. Add the end-card text.
  4. Point at Export. It is not pressed.

Honest note in the video: three touches in the finished ad were done outside solorender. They are the title playing inside the laptop, the bouncing captions and the 2.4× speed-up on the bike. The separate finishing guide covers those.
