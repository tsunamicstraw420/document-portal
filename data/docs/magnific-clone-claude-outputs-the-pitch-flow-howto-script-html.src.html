How "The Pitch" was built in Flow

On the left is what's on screen; on the right is what Ruby says. Words in orange are typed on screen while she reads them out, word for word. 524 words (about 3 minutes 10 seconds of talking), so roughly 4 minutes once the results play.

"Solo render" is written as two words so the voice says it right. That spelling worked in the Pembrook video, and I'll check it by ear again.

A teenager pitches a hamster heist movie to three studio bosses. Every picture, voice and clip in that ad was made in Solo render, and the heart of it lives on one Flow canvas. Let me show you how it's built.

2 · The map

Your whole canvas 257; the pointer runs along each row
Here's the whole canvas. Two rows. The top row is the boardroom: the pictures, the voices and the talking shots. The bottom row is Milo's trailer: the heist. Each box holds a picture or makes one thing, and the wires carry each result into the next box.

4 · Fix the picture, then animate it

Points at the first laser picture
Now the heist. Here's my first laser shot. Look closely: a beam hits him. Don't animate a mistake. Fixing a picture costs less than half the price of redoing a clip.
Adds an Edit box, wires the picture in, types the fix
Add an Edit box, wire the picture in, and type: Move the red laser beams so that NONE of them touches the hamster: every beam passes clearly above him as he crouches low and squeezes underneath them. Also put a small silver stethoscope back around his neck. Keep everything else exactly the same.
▶ Run, and the fixed picture appears
Run it. Now the beams pass above and behind him.
Adds an Animate box, picks Grok, wires, types
Now an Animate box. Pick Grok Video 1.5 Lite: low-priced clips, with sound. Wire in the fixed picture and type: Low camera at floor level with a slow push-in. The hamster creeps forward under the laser web, belly low to the floor, paws reaching ahead one at a time, eyes glancing up at the beams. The red laser beams stay perfectly still above and behind him and never touch him. SFX: a low electric laser hum, tiny paw taps on the polished floor. No music. No subtitles.
▶ Run, and the clip plays
Run. (The laser clip plays with its sound.)
