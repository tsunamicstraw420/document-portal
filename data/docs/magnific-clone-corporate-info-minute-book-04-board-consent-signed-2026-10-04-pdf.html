Action by Written Consent of the Sole Director
Solorender, Inc., a California corporation

The undersigned, being the sole director of Solorender, Inc. (the "Corporation") named in its
Articles of Incorporation, adopts the following resolutions by written consent under Section 307(b)
of the California Corporations Code, effective October 4, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

1. Articles of Incorporation. RESOLVED, that the Articles of Incorporation filed with the
California Secretary of State on September 30, 2026 (Entity No. B20260442610) are accepted, and
the Secretary shall place a copy in the minute book.

2. Bylaws. RESOLVED, that the Bylaws presented with this consent are adopted as the Bylaws of
the Corporation, and the Secretary shall place them in the minute book.

3. Officers. RESOLVED, that Edmond E. Barna is appointed Chief Executive Officer, President,
Chief Financial Officer and Secretary of the Corporation, to serve at the pleasure of the Board.

4. Principal office. RESOLVED, that the principal executive office of the Corporation is 1792
Harmony Way, Pittsburg, CA 94565.

5. Technology assignment. RESOLVED, that the Corporation enter into the Technology
Assignment Agreement with Edmond E. Barna, in the form presented with this consent, under
which he assigns to the Corporation the solorender software, name, domain, accounts and related
assets (the "Assigned Technology"); and that the Corporation assume his obligations to
solorender's existing customers, including unused purchased credits, from the Effective Date.

6. Founder shares. RESOLVED, that the Corporation sell and issue 8,000,000 shares of its
common stock to Edmond E. Barna at $0.00001 per share, for an aggregate purchase price of
$80.00, under the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement in the form presented with this consent. The
Board determines that the Assigned Technology has a fair value of at least $80.00 and is adequate
consideration for the shares under Section 409. Upon issuance the shares are fully paid and
nonassessable and fully vested.

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the shares are uncertificated, and the Secretary shall send the holder
the written statement the Code requires and record the issuance in the share register.

FURTHER RESOLVED, that the shares are offered and sold in reliance on Section 4(a)(2) of the
Securities Act of 1933 and Section 25102(f) of the California Corporate Securities Law of 1968,
and the officers are authorized to file any notice those exemptions require and pay the related fees.

7. Fiscal year. RESOLVED, that the fiscal year of the Corporation ends on December 31.

8. Tax status. RESOLVED, that the Corporation will be taxed as a C corporation, and no officer
shall file an election to be treated as an S corporation (IRS Form 2553) without the Board's prior
approval.

9. Tax ID and filings. RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to obtain an Employer
Identification Number from the Internal Revenue Service, to file the Corporation's Statement of
Information with the California Secretary of State within 90 days after the filing date and each
year after, and to make any other tax or government registration the Corporation's business
requires.

10. Bank accounts. RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to open deposit and payment
accounts in the Corporation's name at any bank or payment processor they choose, including
Stripe, and to sign the institution's standard account resolutions; and that any officer may sign
checks, transfers and withdrawals from those accounts.

11. Organization expenses. RESOLVED, that the Corporation reimburse Edmond E. Barna for
the $105.00 he paid the California Secretary of State to file the Articles of Incorporation and
obtain a certified copy, and for other documented organization expenses; and that the Corporation
may elect to deduct organization and start-up expenses as the Internal Revenue Code permits.

12. General authority. RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to take all further actions and
sign all further documents needed to carry out these resolutions, and all actions already taken
consistent with them are ratified.
