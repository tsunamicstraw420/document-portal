Technology Assignment Agreement
This Technology Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of October 4, 2026 (the
"Effective Date") between Edmond E. Barna ("Assignor"), of 1792 Harmony Way, Pittsburg, CA
94565, and Solorender, Inc., a California corporation (the "Company").

Background. Assignor created and operates solorender, an AI image, video and audio studio at
solorender.com, earlier developed under the name "onda." The Company was formed to own and
run it. In exchange for shares of the Company, Assignor transfers solorender to the Company.

1. Assignment. Assignor irrevocably assigns to the Company all of Assignor's right, title and
interest worldwide in the Assigned Technology. This includes all copyrights, trade secrets,
trademark rights and goodwill, domain names and other intellectual property rights in it, and all
rights to sue for past, present and future infringement.

2. Assigned Technology. "Assigned Technology" means everything listed in Exhibit A and all
other software, content, data, brands and materials Assignor created or acquired for solorender (or
onda) before the Effective Date.

3. Accounts. For each account listed in Exhibit A, Assignor will, within 60 days after the Effective
Date, transfer the account to the Company or change its legal owner and billing to the Company,
as the provider allows. Until then Assignor holds each account for the Company's benefit. Prepaid
balances in those accounts pass to the Company.

4. Customers. From the Effective Date, the Company assumes Assignor's obligations to
solorender's existing customers, including honoring unused purchased credits and active
subscriptions.

5. Consideration. Assignor makes this assignment in exchange for the 8,000,000 shares of the
Company's common stock issued under the Founder Stock Purchase Agreement of the same date.
No other payment is owed.

6. Assignor's statements. Assignor states that:

 1. he created the Assigned Technology himself, including with AI-assisted coding tools, or
    otherwise has the right to assign it;
 2. to his knowledge, no other person or entity owns or claims any interest in it; and
 3. he has granted no license, lien or other right in it that remains in effect, other than the terms of
    service solorender offers its customers.

Third-party software, open-source components, AI models and online services that solorender uses
remain subject to their own licenses and terms. They are not assigned, except for Assignor's rights
under those licenses to the extent they can be transferred.
7. Further assurances. Assignor will sign any further documents and take any further actions the
Company reasonably requests to record or complete this assignment.

8. Moral rights. To the extent the law allows, Assignor waives any moral rights in the Assigned
Technology.

9. General. California law governs this Agreement. It is the entire agreement on its subject and
may be changed only in writing signed by both parties. It may be signed electronically and in
counterparts.
