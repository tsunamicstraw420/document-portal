Founder Stock Purchase Agreement
This Founder Stock Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of October 4, 2026 (the
"Effective Date") between Solorender, Inc., a California corporation (the "Company"), and
Edmond E. Barna ("Purchaser"), of 1792 Harmony Way, Pittsburg, CA 94565.

1. Purchase and sale. The Company sells, and Purchaser buys, 8,000,000 shares of the Company's
common stock (the "Shares") at $0.00001 per share, for a total purchase price of $80.00.

2. Payment. Purchaser pays the purchase price by assigning the solorender technology, name,
domain, accounts and related assets to the Company under the Technology Assignment Agreement
of the same date. The Company's Board of Directors has determined that this consideration has a
fair value of at least $80.00.

3. Closing. The sale closes on the Effective Date, once this Agreement and the Technology
Assignment Agreement are both signed. The Shares are uncertificated. The Company records them
in its share register and sends Purchaser the written statement the California Corporations Code
requires.

4. Vesting. The Shares are fully vested when issued. The Company has no right to repurchase
them.

5. Purchaser's statements. Purchaser states that:

 1. he is buying the Shares for his own account, for investment, and not with a view to distributing
    them;
 2. he understands the Shares are not registered under the Securities Act of 1933 or qualified
    under California law, are sold under exemptions from those requirements, and cannot be resold
   unless they are registered and qualified or an exemption applies;
 3. as the Company's founder, sole director and only officer, he has all the information about the
    Company he considers necessary, and he can bear the economic risk of losing his entire
    investment; and
 4. he understands the Shares carry the legend in Section 6.

6. Legend. The Company will include the following legend in the written statement for the Shares
and on any certificate later issued for them:
        THE SHARES HAVE NOT BEEN REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS
        AMENDED, OR QUALIFIED UNDER ANY STATE SECURITIES LAW. THEY MAY NOT BE
        SOLD, PLEDGED OR OTHERWISE TRANSFERRED WITHOUT AN EFFECTIVE
        REGISTRATION AND QUALIFICATION OR AN AVAILABLE EXEMPTION FROM THOSE
        REQUIREMENTS.
7. Taxes. Purchaser has had the opportunity to consult his own tax advisers and is responsible for
his own taxes. Because the Shares are fully vested when issued, no election under Section 83(b) of
the Internal Revenue Code is needed.

8. General. California law governs this Agreement. Together with the Technology Assignment
Agreement, it is the entire agreement on its subject and may be changed only in writing signed by
both parties. It binds and benefits the parties' successors and permitted assigns. It may be signed
electronically and in counterparts.
