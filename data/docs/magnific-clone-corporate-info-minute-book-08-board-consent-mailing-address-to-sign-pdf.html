The undersigned, being the sole director of Solorender, Inc. (the "Corporation") named in its

of the California Corporations Code, effective October 13, 2026.

office and the address of its agent for service of process remain 1792 Harmony Way, Pittsburg, CA

to the California Secretary of State in the Corporation's Statement of Information, and to the
