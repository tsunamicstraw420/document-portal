FAQ

Who can join? Anyone with an audience — creators, newsletter writers, YouTubers, TikTokers, community owners. No minimum follower count.

When do I get paid? Monthly, via Stripe or PayPal, once your balance reaches $50. There's a 30-day pending period so refunds can settle first.

How long does the cookie last? 60 days from the click. If they buy within that window, the referral is yours.

How long do I earn on a referral? 12 months from the day they sign up — on every pack they buy, not just the first.

Can I refer myself? No — self-referrals and fraudulent traffic void commissions. Everything's tracked on settled payments, so chargebacks are clawed back.

Do I need to be a Studio Partner? No, these are separate. Anyone can be an affiliate. Studio Partners are a small, invite-only group who also get free credits and early model access — and they keep an affiliate link too.
