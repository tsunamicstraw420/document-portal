Can we keep selling "ElevenLabs Music"?

ElevenLabs changed its music rules on 9 Oct 2026. The new rules stop people from reselling its music model next to other companies' models without written permission. We buy this model through fal and list it in our model picker. This page checks whether the new rule reaches us. Everything below comes from free reading of the live pages today. Nothing was changed on the site and nothing was spent.

Short answer

Yes, it very likely reaches us. It reaches us through fal, not directly.

  • We have no deal with ElevenLabs for music, so the rules don't bind us directly. fal is ElevenLabs' customer.
  • But the rules forbid fal from letting anyone else resell the model. And fal's own rules say we must follow the model maker's terms.
  • What we do matches ElevenLabs' definition of "Model Resale" almost word for word. We list ElevenLabs Music beside Lyria, Stable Audio and CassetteAI for our customers to pick and run.
  • It is not an outright ban. It is "not allowed unless ElevenLabs gives written permission." fal may already have that permission. Nothing public says whether it covers fal's customers.

My recommendation: ask fal in writing first (free, quick). If fal can't confirm, hide the model and point music users to the other music models, after checking their terms.

1. What ElevenLabs' new music rules say

Source: elevenlabs.io/music-terms, "Last updated: 09 October 2026", read in full today.

The rule itself — §4.3
"You must not engage in, or permit any third party to engage in, Model Resale, including offering any Music Model alongside third-party models, unless ElevenLabs has expressly authorized that activity in writing."

Strength: needs ElevenLabs' written OK. Note the words "or permit any third party". That is the part that reaches past fal to fal's customers.

What counts as "Model Resale" — §4.8(e)
"any resale, repackaging, redistribution, aggregation, or other making available of Music Models through your or any third-party platform or application"

Our model picker makes the model available through our platform. It also offers it "alongside third-party models", the exact example in §4.3.

The one exception, and why it doesn't fit us — §4.8(i)

A company is not a "Reseller":

"solely by generating and distributing Output through its own product, provided it does not make the Music Models or API access to them available to any third party"

That covers an app that uses the model behind the scenes, for example to add background music to its own videos. We do the opposite. Our customers pick "ElevenLabs Music" by name and run it themselves. That is making the model available to third parties, so the exception most likely does not cover us.

Who the rules bind — opening paragraph
"govern your … use of the Music Service, whether you use it on its own or as a component of another ElevenLabs Service."

"Component" means part of another ElevenLabs product. It does not mean part of someone else's app like ours. The rules add on to "your written agreement with ElevenLabs or … the ElevenLabs Terms of Service". The party bound is ElevenLabs' own customer. For our music, that customer is fal, not us.

Plan table — Exhibit A

The "Model Resale" row says "Prohibited" on every self-serve plan, Free through Business. It says "Custom" on the two Enterprise plans. Reselling is only on the table through an enterprise deal.

If we became an authorized reseller — §4.6 and §4.8(b)

An "Authorized Reseller" is one with "prior express written authorization" (§4.8(b)). It "shall prominently co-brand any offering that incorporates or is built upon the Music Service", for example "powered by ElevenLabs" (§4.6). Permission would come with a co-branding duty.

When it takes effect — §5.2
"An update that materially reduces your rights takes effect 30 days after ElevenLabs notifies you"

If ElevenLabs told its customers on 9 Oct, the resale rule would apply to them from about 8 Nov 2026. That is my reading of the date. I could not see when, or whether, fal was notified.

Other music rules that travel with the model
  • §2.1: prompts must not name any artist, songwriter, song, album, publisher or label, or quote distinctive lyrics.
  • §4.2: Output can't go into a "Music Library" without written OK. §4.8(f) defines that as a collection made available to others "to use, license, download, or incorporate into their content".

I did not check whether any ElevenLabs music sits in our Prompt Library previews.

2. What fal's rules say

Sources: fal.ai/terms ("Last Updated: September 8, 2026"), fal API Services Terms, fal Acceptable Use Policy, fal model page elevenlabs/music/v2.5, fal ElevenLabs page.

fal Terms §14(b) — model makers' terms apply to us
"Your access to and use of such Third-Party Materials may be subject to additional terms, conditions, and policies (including terms of service or privacy policies of the providers of such Third-Party Materials)."

§14(a) defines Third-Party Materials to include "machine learning models hosted by third parties". The same §14(b) also says fal "may block or disable access to any Third-Party Materials … at any time". So if ElevenLabs objects, fal can switch the model off for us without notice.

fal Acceptable Use Policy, "Compliance" — the strongest link

It says not to use fal's services in any way:

"that breaches fal's Terms of Service, including without limitation this AUP, or any third party terms related to the use of third party products and services."

Breaking ElevenLabs' music rules would also break fal's rules. fal's rules do bind us directly.

fal API Services Terms — reselling to our customers is allowed in general

Services item 1 lets us "allow End Users to access and use the Company Platform through the Client Solution". That is fal's general permission to build an app like ours on top of fal. Item 5 ("End User Agreements") makes us bind our customers to terms "as protective of the Services" as fal's. fal Terms §4(b) says the same for apps built on fal's API.

None of this overrides a model maker's own limit. §14(b) and the AUP bring that limit back in.

fal's ElevenLabs Music page

The model page carries the tags "Commercial use" and "Partner". It has no terms of its own. fal's ElevenLabs page says only that content "can be used in commercial projects" and to check fal's terms. That is about using the music commercially. It says nothing about reselling the model.

My reading of the "Partner" tag: fal's API Services Terms item 3 says fal "will identify on the Company Platform which AI models are accessed via such a third-party API partner". So the tag likely means fal runs this model through ElevenLabs' own API, which makes fal an ElevenLabs customer. That is an inference, not a stated fact.

3. Does fal have permission from ElevenLabs?

  • fal announced a partnership with ElevenLabs on 27 Feb 2025 "to bring their complete suite of state-of-the-art audio AI models to the fal platform" (fal blog).
  • fal lists ElevenLabs Music next to hundreds of other models. By §4.3 that is itself Model Resale. So fal either has ElevenLabs' written OK or is breaking the rule too. A formal partnership makes the first more likely.
  • Unknown: whether that OK also covers fal's customers who resell the model in their own apps. Nothing public says so. Only fal or ElevenLabs can answer.
