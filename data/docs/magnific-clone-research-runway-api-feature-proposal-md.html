Runway API research → solorender feature proposal

Research date: 2026-09-11. Sources: Runway's developer docs (docs.dev.runwayml.com) and fal.ai model pages, all fetched that day; URLs cited inline. Runway is studied for product LOGIC only — solorender buys all generation wholesale from fal and never routes through Runway.

A useful coincidence: Runway's API credit is worth $0.01 — exactly the same as a solorender credit. Every Runway price below is directly comparable to solorender's menu, which makes it easy to sanity-check whether a feature stays competitive after the 3× markup.

1. What Runway's API offers, in plain English

Grouped by capability. Sources: docs homepage, models guide, pricing guide, API usage guide, SDK reference.

A. Make images from text (with reference images). Runway resells a familiar roster: Gen-4 Image, GPT Image 2/2.5, Seedream 5, Gemini image models, Grok Imagine, plus a cheap house model. One notable product idea: tagged reference images — upload reference photos, name them with @-tags ("@Sarah", "@MyProduct"), then prompt "@Sarah holding @MyProduct on a beach."

B. Make videos from text or an image. Standard text→video and image→video through one endpoint. Roster: Gen-4.5 / Gen-4 Turbo, Veo 3.1 (+Fast), Seedance 2/2.5, MiniMax H3 Max / Hailuo 3, Wan 3, Grok, Gemini Omni Flash. Priced per second, per resolution.

C. Edit an existing video with words ("Aleph"). Runway's headline differentiator: give it a finished VIDEO plus an instruction — "make it winter", "change her jacket to red", "turn this into anime" — and get the same shot back, transformed. Model aleph2, 28 credits/sec ($0.28/s retail). Hailuo 3, Seedance 2 and Gemini Omni Flash also accept video input.

D. Character performance and live avatars. Two things: Act-Two (act_two, 5 cr/s) — film yourself acting, upload it plus a picture of a character, and the character replays your performance (face, expression, gesture). And real-time avatars (gwm1_avatars) — live conversational characters that join video meetings and call tools; an interactive-agent platform, not a render button.

E. Enhance what you already made. Image upscale (magnific_precision_upscaler_v2 — Runway literally sells a Magnific-branded upscaler, 25 cr/image), video upscale (magnific_video_upscaler_creative, per output frame: $0.007 @720p ≈ $0.17/s retail at 24fps, max 30s), SDR→HDR (ruby), and pro delivery formats (ProRes, PNG/EXR, HDR10/HLG).

F. Audio. A resold ElevenLabs suite plus ByteDance Seed Audio: text-to-speech in 70+ languages, text-to-sound-effects, voice isolation, dubbing, speech-to-speech.

G. Recipes — pre-packaged workflows. Fixed-price one-call templates: product ad (200 cr for 4s @720p), product swap (212 cr), product UGC (192 cr), multi-shot video, ad localization, marketing stock imagery, product campaign images. Businesses buy outcomes, not model access.

H. Model routers. An "auto" layer that picks the concrete model per request, with configurable pools.

I. Platform plumbing. Async task queue, content moderation, org billing, usage reporting — solorender's equivalents already exist (credits ledger, refunds on failure, render-job queue).

3. Gaps worth imitating, ranked by product value

Effort scale: small = one new tool card reusing existing plumbing; medium = one new input type (audio or video upload) or a new Flow node; large = a new subsystem.

Gap 1 — Talking characters (lip sync + photo-to-speaker + voices). The user picks any face from their Library, types or uploads what it should say, and gets a video of that person speaking — UGC ads, explainers, characters that talk. The biggest new use case on the list; Runway treats it as a whole product area. fal supply (verified): fal-ai/bytedance/omnihuman/v1.5 at $0.16/s (fal) for photo+audio→talking video; veed/lipsync at $0.40/minute (fal) to re-sync an existing clip's mouth to new audio (≈2 cr/s retail — extremely margin-friendly; premium tiers fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v2/v3 exist); fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3 (fal) for the voice itself. Effort: medium (audio upload/generation, video-as-source). Lives in: a "Talk" group in the Edit lane + a "Speak" node in Flow feeding Render-film.

Gap 2 — Video Upscale ("Magnific for video"). One click on any Library clip returns it sharper and bigger — the founding brand promise extended to video; Runway sells this as the Magnific Video Upscaler at ~$0.17/s retail. fal supply (verified): fal-ai/topaz/upscale/video — $0.01/s up to 720p, $0.02/s to 1080p, $0.08/s above (fal); generative alternative fal-ai/seedvr/upscale/video. At 3×, a 5s 1080p upscale ≈ 30 cr — cheap for the user, healthy margin, far under Runway. Effort: small-medium (no prompt, no mask; Library clips already have URLs, so a Library button needs no upload plumbing). Lives in: Library button first, Edit-lane card later.

Gap 3 — Video editing with words (the Aleph move). Change a finished clip by typing — swap the outfit, change the season, restyle to animation — instead of re-rolling. fal supply (verified): decart/lucy-edit/pro — $0.15/s @720p, $0.10/s @480p (fal); alternatives decart/lucy-edit/fast, fal-ai/luma-dream-machine/ray-2/modify, fal-ai/wan-vace-apps/video-edit. Lucy Pro at 3× is $0.45/s vs Runway's $0.28/s retail — so lead with the budget tiers and keep Pro premium. Effort: medium (same source+prompt shape as the Edit lane, but the source is a video). Lives in: Edit lane video mode; in Flow it un-dead-ends the Animate node.

Gap 4 — Expand an image beyond its borders (outpaint/reframe). Turn a square generation into a widescreen banner or story crop with the AI painting the missing surroundings — a classic Magnific/Photoshop-Generative-Expand power. fal supply (verified): fal-ai/bria/expand (fal), fal-ai/ideogram/v3/reframe, fal-ai/image-apps-v2/outpaint (per-edge control). Effort: small — image in, image out, exactly the existing edit-tool plumbing; the ASPECT_RATIOS picker already exists. Lives in: Edit lane card next to Erase/Upscale; also useful in Flow before Animate.

Gap 5 — Recipes: one-click ad & UGC templates. Pick "Product ad", drop in a product photo, get a finished short video — no model knowledge needed. Runway's fixed-price recipes (product_ad 200 cr, product_ugc 192 cr) prove the demand. fal supply: none needed — pure product logic over models solorender already pays for (Seedream edit → Kling/Seedance animate → Director → render worker), i.e. 100% margin on the packaging. Effort: medium (a template button that pre-builds a wired Flow graph + guided fill-in). Lives in: Flow template gallery, later a dashboard "Make an ad" entry.

Gap 6 — Performance transfer (Act-Two proper). Film yourself acting; any character replays it. fal supply (verified): fal-ai/kling-video/v3/standard/motion-control at $0.126/s (fal); also fal-ai/wan/v2.2-14b/animate/move/replace and fal-ai/wan-motion. Ranked lower on economics: Runway retails Act-Two at $0.05/s while fal wholesale starts at $0.126/s — a 3× markup lands ~7× Runway's price. Wait for cheaper supply; ship Gap 1 first, where the economics work.

Gap 7 — Sound effects & narration as Library assets. fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3, /tts/multilingual-v2, fal-ai/elevenlabs/sound-effects/v2 (fal). Small effort for generate-and-download; medium-large once the render worker mixes audio into films. The natural companion to Gaps 1 and 5.

Gap 8 — Extend a clip. fal-ai/pixverse/extend — $0.15–0.40 per 5s by resolution (fal). Small effort, low urgency (10s durations already selectable at generation).

Not worth imitating now: real-time conversational avatars (different product category, no fal wholesale equivalent); SDR→HDR and ProRes/EXR delivery (film-post niche, no verified fal supplier); @-tag reference naming (polish, not a feature — Nano Banana 2 Edit already takes multiple references).

4. Recommended top-5 build order

  1. Image Expand (outpaint) — smallest lift on the list (reuses the edit-tool plumbing exactly) and a signature Magnific-class power users expect from solorender's brand (fal-ai/bria/expand / fal-ai/ideogram/v3/reframe).
  2. Video Upscale — a one-click Library button at $0.01–0.02/s wholesale (Topaz) against Runway's ~$0.17/s retail; it literally extends the founding promise to video (fal-ai/topaz/upscale/video).
  3. Talking characters — lip sync at $0.40/min wholesale plus OmniHuman photo-to-speaker opens the biggest new market (ads, explainers, character content) and feeds straight into Flow films (veed/lipsync, fal-ai/bytedance/omnihuman/v1.5, fal-ai/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v3).
  4. Video editing with words — the Aleph-style "change my clip by typing" lane turns every past generation into raw material instead of a re-roll, and un-dead-ends Flow's Animate node (decart/lucy-edit/pro + a budget tier).
  5. Flow recipes ("Make an ad") — packages models solorender already pays for into outcomes businesses actually buy, at 100% margin on the packaging; Runway's fixed-price recipes prove the demand (no new fal endpoint needed).

Runners-up: sound effects/narration (bundle into #3 and Render-film), clip extend (cheap button, anytime), performance transfer (wait for cheaper fal supply — wholesale is currently ~7× Runway's retail).
