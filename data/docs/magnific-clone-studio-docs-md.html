solorender — how it's wired (money & machinery)

Internal reference. Every generation solorender sells is bought wholesale from fal.ai and resold as credits. Tables below are derived from the live product config (src/lib/config.ts) joined with fal costs verified from fal's own model pages — regenerate, don't hand-edit.

Last verified: 2026-09-18. Catalog = 68 user-facing models (+2 "Auto" pickers): the six roadmap features (expand, video upscale, extend, edit-by-words, talking photos, recipes) shipped this date; every new model's fal cost was verified live from its model page and OpenAPI schema.

1. The money, in one rule

solorender credits = fal wholesale $ × 3 ÷ $0.01 — 1 credit = $0.01 sticker (500 = $5). ~67% gross margin on everything. Floors on the cheapest models push margin higher (they can't be priced below 3 credits); token-billed models (GPT Image 2) are priced against top quality; uploads are downscaled to 2048px to bound per-megapixel tools.

Video prices scale with user choices. Tables show the base clip; longer length multiplies credits linearly (rounded up); resolution applies a verified multiplier (Seedance 2.5 1080p ×2.15 · H3 480P ×0.63 · PixVerse v6 ladder) and only exists where fal's tier price is verified — everything else stays pinned. Audio, music and lip-sync also scale by the unit fal bills (see §5–§7). The Director (Claude writes the motion prompt from notes + reference images) is a Flow helper at 9 credits, priced near cost; Render film (stitch clips into one MP4) is 5 credits + 1 per clip, also priced near cost. House accounts (owner emails in OWNER_EMAILS) self-grant free credits from the dashboard — the owner pays only the wholesale bill; grants are ledger-logged as "owner grant".

8. Subscription plans & founding offer

Purchases settle to a single credit balance. Credits never expire and roll over. Stripe's fee is charged once per purchase, never per generation. (Stripe is in TEST mode until launch.) Standalone top-up credit packs have been retired — the offer is the subscription plans plus the one-time founding offer.

Subscription plans — a monthly credit stipend plus more storage, more parallel generations, and the pro tools. Annual billing is cheaper per month and grants the full year of credits up front.

10. How a new model gets added

  1. Verify it exists — fal's schema endpoint (fal.ai/api/openapi/queue/openapi.json?endpoint_id=…); null = bad id.
  2. Confirm the input shape — each family maps to a request format in buildInput.
  3. Verify the price — the fal model page states it verbatim ("Your request will cost $X per …"); price at LIST rates, never promos; pin or multiplier-price resolutions; scale durations/characters/seconds by the unit fal bills.
  4. Wire & ship — config entry, type-check, picker check, commit. No paid test runs.

18 image + 11 edit + 25 video + 4 clip tools + 3 voiceover + 3 music + 2 lip-sync + 2 talking photo = 68 models live (+2 "Auto" pickers) · Flow canvas · Director · Render film · Library · house accounts. The ×3 rule and the plumbing hold as the catalog grows. End-user guide: /help in the app (app/(app)/help).
