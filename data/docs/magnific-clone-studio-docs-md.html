1. The money, in one rule

solorender credits = fal wholesale $ × 3 ÷ $0.01 — 1 credit = $0.01 sticker (500 = $5). ~67% gross margin on generation. Standing discount: image edit/upscale tools and video clip tools are priced at wholesale × 2 (~50% margin) instead of × 3 — each shows a "% off" tag (always 33%; never the word "Discounted" — Eddie, 2026-10-11) in the Studio picker, its selected-model button and Flow's model menus, with the pre-discount price struck through (one decimal, so 5 cr shows "was 7.5", not a rounded 8); a fal sale shows its own % off the same way (percentOff() / wasCredits()). The announcement bar lists EVERY discounted model (fal sales first, paused models skipped), rebuilt from the live price list on each page load. Floors on the cheapest models push margin higher (the cheapest discounted tools floor at 2 credits); token-billed models (GPT Image 2 / 2.5) are priced against top quality; uploads are downscaled to 2048px to bound per-megapixel tools.

Video prices scale with user choices. Tables show the base clip; longer length multiplies credits linearly (rounded up); resolution applies a verified multiplier (Seedance 2.5 1080p ×2.46 · H3 480P ×0.63 · PixVerse v6 / Wan 3.0 / Omni Flash ladders) and only exists where fal's tier price is verified — everything else stays pinned. Audio, music and lip-sync also scale by the unit fal bills (see §5–§7). The Director (Claude writes the motion prompt from notes + the keyframe) is the ✨ Direct button: free to press, 9 credits once on the Run of the box it wrote (priced near cost; 25 free presses/day for accounts that have bought credits, 3 for sign-up-only — the allowance is what bounds its cost now); Render film (stitch clips into one MP4) is 5 credits + 1 per clip, also priced near cost. House accounts (owner emails in OWNER_EMAILS) self-grant free credits from the dashboard — the owner pays only the wholesale bill; grants are ledger-logged as "owner grant".

Standalone top-up credit packs have been retired — the offer is the subscription plans plus the one-time founding offer.

Plan credits pause when the plan ends (Eddie, 2026-10-10). Credits granted by a plan's invoice.paid (ledger reason stripe:sub:<invoice>) are counted in profiles.plan_credits, a part of credits, and are spent first. When the subscriptions row's status leaves active/trialing (canceled, past_due, unpaid…), a trigger moves what's left of them out of credits into profiles.paused_credits; back to active, they move back. So credits always means "usable now" — spending, refunds and every balance display work unchanged. Credits bought outright (founders offer, Super Max, top-up packs), signup, refunds and admin grants never pause. Refund/chargeback take-backs take paused credits first, then usable plan credits. A generation refund comes back as always-usable credits (in the customer's favour — a refund isn't traced to which part it was charged from). The webhook never lets an inactive subscription overwrite a different active one (overlapping plans). Credits page, Studio/recipe "not enough" messages, the generate 402 and the admin customer page show the paused amount. SQL lives in supabase/subscriptions.sql (columns, sync_plan_credits, trigger) plus the edited spend_credits, spend_credits_for, grant_credits, settle_purchase_reversal. Applied live 2026-10-10 through the Management API (the MCP declined it), so it is not in list_migrations; dry-run tested on a rolled-back transaction first (14 lifecycle steps). Cancelling always runs to the end of the paid period (Eddie: "needs to stay that way") — /api/portal opens Stripe's portal with our own API-created configuration (metadata solorender: "1", found again on later opens): cancel at_period_end, invoice history, card, email/address; no plan switching. Not a dashboard setting, so it can't be flipped there.

10. How a new model gets added

  1. Verify it exists and is live — fal's catalog API: api.fal.ai/v1/models?endpoint_id=… returns metadata.status (active / deprecated), category and dates (no key needed; ~1 req/s or it 429s). Schema: fal.ai/api/openapi/queue/openapi.json?endpoint_id=…; null = bad id.
  2. Confirm the input shape — each family maps to a request format in buildInput. Check the DEFAULTS too: a flag you don't send (audio on, rendering speed, resolution) is what fal bills.
  3. Verify the price — the model page's publicEndpointBilling JSON gives the headline unit/price; the pricing paragraph gives the tiers (audio on/off, resolution). Price the tier the app actually REQUESTS, at LIST rates, never promos; pin or multiplier-price resolutions; scale durations/characters/seconds by the unit fal bills.
  4. Wire & ship — config entry, type-check, picker check, /help + this doc, commit. No paid test runs.

Lesson 2026-09-20: two models were quietly under-priced for weeks because the price was read off a cheaper tier than the request asked for (Seedance 2.5 at 480p while sending 720p; Ideogram Character at TURBO while fal defaulted to BALANCED), and two were over-priced off stale numbers. Nothing re-checks fal after a model is wired — hence the weekly model-scout task.

Lesson 2026-09-24: the scout's "new on fal" section only reports models listed since the last run, so a model that launched earlier and was never added stayed invisible forever. model-scout.py now also carries a named WATCHLIST (section 5 of the report) — models we have decided not to carry yet, checked by regex against endpoint id AND display name every run, so they stay on the report until they are added to config.ts or deleted from the list. 12. fal concurrency (read 2026-10-09)

fal counts only jobs in progress against an account-wide limit. Extra jobs wait in fal's queue and are never dropped. New accounts get 2. The limit rises automatically with paid credit invoices from the last 4 weeks, self-serve up to 40. Above that (or for dedicated per-model capacity) you talk to sales. fal can also cap busy models separately. The current limit is shown only on the dashboard Concurrency page (fal.ai/dashboard/usage-billing/concurrency), not via API. A video holds its slot for the whole render (2–10 min), so about limit videos at once fill the account. Images then queue inside our fal.subscribe request and can hit the route's time limit (refunded). Fixed 2026-10-09 — we never refund a job fal may still bill: every job is sent with fal's startTimeout: videos 900 s (VIDEO_START_TIMEOUT_S), images/audio 150 s.
