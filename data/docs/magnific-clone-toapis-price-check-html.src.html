How to read this

  • Only some ToAPIs models carry an "-official" name: Veo, Nano Banana / Gemini image, GPT Image and Grok video. Kling, Seedance, Seedream and FLUX have just one listing each, so they sit in "other listing". The Kling v3 prices I described earlier as an "official route" have no such label.
  • Nano Banana and GPT Image "-official" prices are ToAPIs' own per-picture estimates. Those routes bill by tokens, so the real bill can differ. Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 are token-billed too ("from" estimates).
  • Several cheap listings don't say whether sound is included. The cheap Veo price ($0.14 for 8 s) is one of them.
  • "Runs, 30 days" counts Solorender's own creations table. Every model's runs came from a single account. The dollar figures in the summary are estimates from those runs (credits ÷ 2.5).
  • ToAPIs credits: $1 = 200. Prices on the dashboard say "price multiplier is 1x" for the default user group, so a bigger account could be quoted lower.
