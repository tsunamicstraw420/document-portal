Adding the music and effects — step by step

Follow in order. Roughly 10 minutes. Nothing here can break your timeline; if something goes wrong, Cmd+Z undoes it.

Work on the Edit page (the icon at the bottom of the screen that looks like a small filmstrip with a scissors).

Part 1 — Bring the two files in

1. Menu bar → File → Import → Media…

2. Go to 90Day_LastResort_Season3 → roughcut and select both:

  • EP-02_MUSIC_STEM.wav
  • EP-02_SFX_STEM.wav

Click Open.

3. They appear in the Media Pool, top-left. They're large files — Resolve may take a few seconds to show a waveform. That's normal.

If a box asks about changing the frame rate, click Don't change.

Part 2 — Make two empty audio tracks

Right now you have one audio track (Ruby's narration). You need two more.

4. Menu bar → Timeline → Add Track → Add Audio Track

5. Do it again. Menu bar → Timeline → Add Track → Add Audio Track

You should now see A1, A2 and A3 stacked at the left of the timeline. A1 is Ruby. A2 and A3 are empty.

If it asks what kind of track, choose Stereo.

Part 3 — Put the music on A2

Ignore the destination-selector method. Arming a destination track in Resolve is fussy and it kept sending the clip to A1. Drag and snap instead — the start of the timeline is a hard snap point, so you can't land a frame off.

6. Turn snapping on. Press the N key, or click the small magnet icon in the toolbar above the timeline. When it's on, the magnet is highlighted.

7. In the Media Pool, click and hold EP-02_MUSIC_STEM.wav and drag it down onto the empty A2 track. Drop it anywhere on that row for now.

8. Now drag the clip left until it bumps against the very start of the timeline. With snapping on you'll feel it lock into place and stop.

9. Check it. Press Home — the playhead jumps to 00:00:00:00. The left edge of the music clip should sit exactly on the playhead, no sliver of gap.

Part 4 — Put the effects on A3

10. Drag EP-02_SFX_STEM.wav from the Media Pool onto the empty A3 track.

11. Drag it left until it snaps against the start, same as before.

12. Press Home again and check the left edge sits on the playhead.

Both stems are now in place.

If a clip refuses to snap all the way left, something's already sitting on that track — look for a tiny clip fragment and delete it, then try again.

Part 5 — Check it worked

14. Look at the right-hand end of the timeline. The two stems should finish together at 00:19:10:13, slightly past Ruby's narration on A1 (which ends at 19:05) — that gap is the endcard. If a stem ends noticeably early or late, it didn't snap to zero; undo and redo Part 3.

The track headers should now read Audio 2 — 1 Clip and Audio 3 — 1 Clip instead of 0 Clips. That's the quickest confirmation both landed.

15. Now spot-check four moments. Type the timecode into the box at the top-left of the timeline and press Enter, then play a few seconds:

Go toYou should hear
00:00:00:00Music starting immediately, with punch
00:02:16:00A stamp hit as the MAY 20 card appears
00:02:40:00No music at all — this is deliberate, the arrest sequence
00:18:08:00A big impact as the 8/10 badge appears

If the stamp at 2:16 doesn't line up with the card, tell me — don't try to fix it by dragging.

Part 6 — Set the levels

16. Play a normal stretch — try 00:05:00:00. Listen to whether Ruby sits clearly on top of the music.

17. If the music is too loud, drag the volume line across the middle of the music clip on A2 downward. A tooltip shows the change in dB. Try −3 dB at a time.

18. Same for the effects on A3 if they're jumping out.

Both stems were built with sensible levels already (music around −20 dB, effects around −13 dB), so you may not need to touch anything.

Part 7 — Ducking (optional, do it last)

Ducking makes the music dip automatically whenever Ruby speaks. It's the single biggest improvement to a mix — but it's fiddly, and the music is already quiet enough that you might not need it.

Try the episode without it first. If the music feels like it's competing with her, come back and tell me, and I'll walk you through the sidechain setup on the Fairlight page as its own thing.

When you're done

Save with Cmd+S, then tell me. Next step is export.
