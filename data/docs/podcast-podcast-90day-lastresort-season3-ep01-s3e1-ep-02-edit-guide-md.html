EP-02 (Last Resort S3E1) — DaVinci Resolve Edit Guide

Everything's generated. This is how to assemble it in DaVinci Resolve (free is fine) → 1080p export. Lip-sync is skipped for this episode — Ruby's loops play as-is under her narration.

Project settings

  • Timeline: 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps.
  • Import everything from this folder: videos/, overlays/, audio/.

1. Lay the spine: Ruby's narration

  • Drop audio/LastResort_03_01B.m4a on an audio track. This is the master — everything syncs to it. (Full transcript for reference: lipsync/01B_transcript.txt.)

2. Place the video clips over the narration

Follow the shot order in EP-02_RUN_SHEET.md (S1–S8, then SC-1…SC-19), cutting to each clip on its narration beat. Two clip types:

  • RUBY cutaways — use the loop for that segment's set: booth = videos/RL-1_booth_loop.mp4 (or RL-2_booth_reaction for sarcasm, RL-6_booth_reaction for shock), glam = RL-3_glam_loop, cozy = RL-4_cozy_loop, neon = RL-5_neon_loop. Reuse freely; trim each to the line.
  • SCENE / B-roll — the S0 and SC- clips. SC-01_therapy_circle is reusable B-roll you can drop under several couple mentions.

Ruby cutaway timings are pre-computed in lipsync/cues.csv (clip_id, which loop, start/end seconds in the narration, and the line). Use it to place each Ruby beat exactly; adjust by ear.

4. Music + SFX

Lay a music bed (drama / tension / sentimental as the segment calls for) low under the narration; add stings on the big reactions and card pop-ins.

5. Captions

Skip for now, or add after upload from YouTube's auto-transcript.

6. Export & publish

  • Deliver: H.264, 1080p (free Resolve does this natively).
  • Upload to YouTube unlisted (Wed) → pull the transcript for show notes/chapters/Short captions → flip public (Fri), which fires the publish Zaps.

Later (not now)

  • Lip-sync: free tool (Easy-Wav2Lip) is stale vs current Python and didn't install on your Mac or Colab. Prep is saved (lipsync/ transcript, cues, segments) and ready to feed a newer tool, or ~5 marquee beats can be done on Magnific (~9k credits) if you want that polish on a future pass.
