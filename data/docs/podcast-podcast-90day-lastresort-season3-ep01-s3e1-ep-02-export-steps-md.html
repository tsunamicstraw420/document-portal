Where the meter lives, if you ever want it: Fairlight page, top right, panel headed Loudness. It defaults to BS.1770-1 (LU), which shows relative numbers like "+4.8". Click the ⋯ at that panel's top-right corner to switch the scale to LUFS for absolute readings.

Part 2 — Set up the export

4. Click the Deliver page (the rocket icon, bottom right).

5. Top-left you'll see a row of preset buttons. Click YouTube.

6. Under the preset, choose 1080p.

This fills in the right format automatically: MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio.

7. Check these fields underneath:

FieldShould say
FormatMP4
CodecH.264
Resolution1920 × 1080
Frame rate24
QualityAutomatic (or "Restrict to 20,000 Kb/s")
Audio codecAAC
Audio bitrate320 Kb/s

Frame rate is the one to actually verify. It must be 24 — the same as the timeline. If it offers 23.976, that's not the same thing; set it to 24.

8. In Filename, type: EP-02_LastResort_S3E1

9. Click Browse and choose where to save it. The roughcut folder is fine.

Part 3 — Subtitles: leave them OFF

10. Scroll down the Deliver settings to find Subtitle Settings.

11. Make sure Export Subtitle is unchecked.

This is deliberate. You want to upload the .srt to YouTube separately so viewers can toggle captions on and off, and so YouTube can read the text for search. Burning them in takes that away permanently.

Part 4 — Render

12. Click Add to Render Queue (bottom of the settings panel).

13. The job appears in the Render Queue on the right. Click Render All.

14. Leave it alone. Progress shows in the queue.

Part 5 — Check the file

15. When it finishes, open the MP4 in QuickTime and spot-check:
