Three rules for building future episode timelines

Learned the hard way across four failed imports. All three are enforced in outputs/build_v10_xmeml.py.

1. Use Final Cut 7 XMEML (.xml), not FCPXML (.fcpxml). FCPXML represents extra video tracks as "lanes," and Resolve silently discards them — v9 imported as a single track with the overlays dropped on the floor. XMEML has real <track> elements.

2. A still's <file> block must carry NO <duration>. Declaring one makes Resolve treat the PNG as hours of video: it draws frame 0, hunts for frame 1, finds nothing, and drops the whole track to Media Offline. This looks exactly like a missing file, which is why relinking never fixed it. It was the v9/v10/v11 bug.

3. Keep stills off the video track. The endcard on V1 — a track otherwise full of video clips — was silently dropped (v12 just ended at the last Ruby beat). Moving it to V2 fixed it. Put every still on a stills-only track.

Reference test files: roughcut/_tracktest/ — three tiny 8-second timelines that isolate which dialect Resolve honours. Re-run that probe before trusting any new timeline format.
