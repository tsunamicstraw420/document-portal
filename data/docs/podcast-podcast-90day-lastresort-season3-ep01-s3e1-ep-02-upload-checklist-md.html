EP-02 — upload day checklist

The episode is exported and finished. This is what happens next, in order.

The finished file: roughcut/Last_Resort_S3E1.mp4 The captions file: roughcut/EP-02_v8_CAPTIONS.srt — a separate file. Not inside the video.

Wednesday — upload as Unlisted

1. In YouTube Studio, click Create → Upload video and choose Last_Resort_S3E1.mp4.

2. While it processes, set:

  • Title — leave a placeholder for now, Claude writes the real one from the transcript
  • Visibility → Unlisted (not Public yet, not Private — Claude needs to reach it to pull the transcript)

3. Add the captions. This is the step people forget.

  • In the video's settings, go to Subtitles (left menu, or the "Show more" section).
  • Choose the language (English), then Add → Upload file → With timing.
  • Select EP-02_v8_CAPTIONS.srt.
  • These are cleaner than YouTube's auto-captions — the cast names are corrected and the timing is frame-accurate.

4. Publish as Unlisted, then tell Claude it's up.

Wednesday — Claude's part

Once it's unlisted, Claude will:

  • pull YouTube's transcript
  • write the title, description, chapters (timestamps), and show notes
  • you paste those into the video

Thursday — Shorts

  • Claude cuts the vertical Shorts (see SHORTS_GUIDE.md — the "zero chill" cold open goes first).
  • Claude writes each Short's caption.

Friday — go public

5. Flip the video from Unlisted → Public.

That's the trigger. Publishing fires the Zaps: the RSS feed updates, the Facebook groups get posted, the email goes out.

6. Post the cold-open Short the same day, linked to the full episode.

Why the captions aren't in the video

Burning captions into the picture would make them permanent — no toggle, no way for a viewer to turn them off, and YouTube couldn't read the text for search. Uploading the SRT separately keeps them switchable and searchable. The only place we do burn captions in is the Shorts, because the Shorts player has no caption toggle.
