Why these

I can't hear audio, so I picked on length, title and tags — which covers the objective criteria but not whether they actually sound good. Treat this as a first pass you can veto. The reasoning:

  • Everything is 1–2 seconds. The most common beginner mistake is effects that are too long, so I filtered hard on duration. I skipped every "cinematic whoosh" at 6–8 seconds — they'd still be ringing while Ruby talks.
  • Two different pops. The Shekinah bubbles land 1.5 seconds apart. Slightly different pops make it read as posts piling up rather than a stutter.
  • The stamp is a real rubber stamp, tagged Heavy, Stamping, Thud — a dry mechanical hit, not a boom.
  • The riser is only 2 seconds. Most risers run 5–8 and would start before Ruby's last line ends.
