⚠️ Known clip quirk

19 clips are 1284×716; SC-05 is 1280×720. Neither is exactly 1920×1080, and 1284×716 isn't true 16:9 (1.793 vs 1.778) — so on a 1080p timeline Resolve will scale them slightly differently and SC-05 may look a hair different in framing. Fix: set Project Settings → Image Scaling → Scale entire image to fit, and it's invisible. Only if you spot a size pop on the SC-05 cut, nudge that clip's zoom ~1.01.
