Two rules that cost us four failed imports on EP-02

  1. Stills go on their own track (V2/V3), never on the video track — Resolve silently drops stills sitting among video clips.
  2. If you hand-build a timeline XML, a still's <file> block must carry no <duration> or the whole track goes Media Offline.
