🔒 Captions — the locked workflow (so we stop re-deciding this)

Locked by Eddie, re-confirmed Jul 31 2026. This is the single source of truth. If any older doc disagrees, THIS wins.

We build our OWN captions from the word-level transcript (lipsync/LastResort_03_04_CLEAN.words.json), never YouTube's. There are two caption forms across three destinations:

DestinationVideo fileCaptions
YouTubeEP-04_S3E4_YOUTUBE.mp4Karaoke BURNED IN (white Bangers, spoken word gold) AND upload EP04_S3E4_CAPTIONS.srt separately in the Subtitles box
SpotifyEP-04_S3E4_SPOTIFY.mp4CLEAN — nothing burned, and do not upload the SRT (Spotify auto-captions)
ShortsShorts/*.mp4Their own vertical karaoke burned in (cut from the CLEAN master)
Both main files now carry the 10s neon-logo intro + outro (runtime ~10:00). Because content starts at 0:10, the uploaded .srt was shifted +10s to match. EP-04_S3E4_MASTER.mp4 is the clean episode WITHOUT intro/outro — kept internal (it's the source the Shorts are cut from), don't upload it.

Why burned + SRT on YouTube: karaoke is per-word animation, which a plain .srt can't do — so it must be burned into the picture. We upload the .srt too only so YouTube can index the words (search + the transcript panel). Since the captions are already visible, viewers won't toggle CC on, so there's no double-caption problem in practice.

Why the Shorts are cut from the CLEAN master (not the YouTube file): if we cut them from the burned YouTube file they'd show the episode's captions and the Short's own karaoke — double captions. Always cut Shorts from the clean master.

The files that make this

All in EP04_S3E4/:

  • burn_captions.py → builds captions/EP04_S3E4_KARAOKE.ass (to burn) + captions/EP04_S3E4.srt (to upload) from the word JSON.
  • burn_youtube_chunks.py → burns the .ass onto the clean master → EP-04_S3E4_YOUTUBE.mp4 (chunked + resumable).
  • shorts_pipeline/make_shorts.py → the 7 Shorts, each with its own karaoke.
