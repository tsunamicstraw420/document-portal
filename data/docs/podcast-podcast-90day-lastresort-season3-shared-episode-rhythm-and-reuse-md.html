Reality Recaps Unfiltered — Episode Rhythm & _SHARED Reuse Playbook

Researched Aug 3 2026. The skeleton is identical every episode; only scene content + count of body loops (N = number of couples/storylines) changes.

Pacing principles (sourced)

  • Hook deadline is 15 seconds, not 30. Steepest drop is sec 10–20. Cold-open hook must land by 0:15; logo comes AFTER the hook, kept < ~20s. (We already do cold-open-first.)
  • Two-layer rhythm (the key correction to "20–30s scene / 20–30s Ruby"): Ruby's voice is continuous; only her face alternates in ~once per minute for an 18–25s beat. WITHIN each scene block, cut every 4–7 seconds across 4–6 stills/clips. A 30s block held on one still = the jitter/deadness. A 30s block cut across 4–6 visuals feels alive.
  • Ruby ≤3 min face-time conflicts with a true 1:1 host:scene split, so scene B-roll carries ~8 of ~11 min → ~2.5:1 scene:host ratio. Scene blocks run ~40s (with fast internal cuts), not 20–30s.
  • B-roll shot length in the final edit: 3–7s per clip (never < 4s — our flash-cut rule). Reality/MTV grammar ≈ 4s average shot.
  • Structure as repeating set-up → tension → payoff micro-arcs, one every 1–2 min. Narration ≈ 160 wpm (≈1,750 words for 11 min).
  • Shorts: win/lose in 3s; a clean loop ending is the biggest replay lever (we already do this).

Ken Burns "jitter" fix: tiny 2–3% micro-zooms ARE the jitter. Either (a) one decisive 8–12% move over the full 4–7s, one direction, eased — never a continuous shimmer; or better (b) don't animate most stills — sell "alive" with a comic overlay reveal (panel slam-in, speed lines, quote-bubble pop) on a held still. Reserve real paid animation for the ~6–8 hero beats only.

_SHARED reuse rules

★ STANDING GOAL (Eddie, Aug 15 2026): every new episode must ACCUMULATE clips for future episodes. Reuse is a two-way street — each episode pulls free shots FROM _SHARED and deposits new reusable (non-story) shots back INTO it. After every build, harvest that episode's reusable clips — location/ establishing shots, recurring-couple B-roll, individual portraits, couple references — into _SHARED/scene_library/{locations,couples/<pair>,individuals} (and portraits into _SHARED/couples/), 16:9-verified, v##-named, logged in _MANIFEST.md. The library must GROW every week; the cost model below depends on it compounding (aim for ~10–14 of ~25 shots free once the season matures). Skipping the harvest = throwing away next week's free footage.

Goes in _SHARED (generate once, reuse forever — no week-specific story content): logo stings + Shorts header · host booth base loops (lip-sync is a per-episode swap, not a regen) · transitions/bumpers · overlay templates (lower-thirds, B4 quote bubble, date panel) · establishing/location shots (castle/bar exterior) · music beds + SFX · recurring couple roll-call portraits.

Must be episode-specific (never SHARED): this week's scene/story B-roll · the cold-open hook clip · the deep-dive clip · Ruby's lip-synced booth output for this week · overlay text carrying this week's quotes/dates · a brand-new cast member's portrait (until they recur, then they graduate).

Naming/versioning: SHARED_<category>_<name>_v##.<ext> and EP##_SC##_<desc>_v01.<ext>. Never overwrite — always bump v##. Keep _SHARED/_MANIFEST.md listing the canonical version per asset with a "16:9 verified ✓" box.

_SHARED/
  logo/  host_loops/  transitions/  overlays/  establish/  portraits/  music/  sfx/  _MANIFEST.md

Graduation rule: a clip earns _SHARED when (1) no week-specific story content, (2) used/planned for 2+ episodes, (3) approved + 16:9-verified. Rule of thumb: "references this week's drama = episode-specific; want it again next week = SHARED."

Cost impact

  • _SHARED assets = zero marginal cost (amortized forever): ~10–14 of a ~25-shot episode covered free once the season matures.
  • Fixed skeleton = bounded shot list (generate to template, no over-producing).
  • Ruby booth = lip-sync swap (cheap Kling pool), not regen.
  • Target ~6–10 NEW paid animated clips/episode: cold open + deep dive + one hero beat for the 4–6 biggest storylines. Every other scene = still held 4–7s with a comic overlay reveal (free) instead of paid animation. Establishing/logos/transitions/overlays/music = 0 new (SHARED). → roughly a 60–70% cut in paid generation per episode, with real motion where the retention curve spikes.
