How to write a Lyria prompt

Learned the hard way, July 20 2026.

Do: genre, mood, instruments, tempo. Two or three sentences.

Don't use structural or time-based words. This killed two generations before I spotted the pattern:

  • "opens with 15 seconds of attitude, then pulls back into a loopable bed" → failed
  • "Dark trap intro… a hook that grabs attention immediately" → failed
  • Same prompt with "intro" and "immediately" removed → worked

Lyria doesn't arrange a song, it renders a texture. Words like intro, outro, builds, opens, then, immediately appear to break it. Describe how the music feels and sounds, never what it does over time. (The "outro" prompt below survived with "builds and resolves" intact, so it isn't a hard blocklist — but if a generation fails, strip the time words first.)

Failed generations are not charged.

Two phrases worth keeping in every prompt:

  • "with plenty of open space" — keeps the midrange clear so Ruby's voice sits on top instead of fighting the music
  • "Instrumental only, no vocals" — say it explicitly or you get vocal chops

The library — four tracks, all generated July 20 2026

Eddie chose four over six: handle light and emotional moments by pulling the bed down or out rather than swapping it. Fewer things to get wrong in the mix, and the palette stays consistent.

1. RRU_neutral_bed.mp3 — 2:35 ✅ approved

Dark moody trap instrumental. Slow heavy half-time groove, deep 808 sub bass, crisp rolling hi-hats, sparse minor key synth melody. Confident and understated, with plenty of open space. Instrumental only, no vocals.

The workhorse. Runs under most of the episode.

2. RRU_intro.mp3 — 2:35

Dark energetic trap instrumental. Hard 808 sub bass, fast rolling hi-hats, a bold minor key synth hook. Punchy, bright and full of swagger and attitude. Instrumental only, no vocals.

Full energy, no restraint — nobody talks over this. 00:00–00:06 and the top of every episode.

3. RRU_tension_bed.mp3 — 2:52

Dark tense trap instrumental. Sparse and uneasy, deep droning sub bass, minimal percussion, a single unsettling minor key synth line with lots of silence between notes. Cold and restrained, with plenty of open space. Instrumental only, no vocals.

The heavier material — Shekinah's posts, the confrontation at 06:52, Sarper at 14:40.

4. RRU_outro.mp3 — 2:39

Dark trap outro that builds and resolves. Deep 808 sub bass, rolling hi-hats, a confident minor key synth hook that feels like an ending. Satisfying and complete, warmer than the intro. Instrumental only, no vocals.

19:00 sign-off through the endcard.

If you later want light and emotional beds

Held back deliberately. Both stepped outside the palette — the light bed went major key, the emotional one brought in piano and pads — and risked sounding like a different show against the dark graphic-novel artwork. If the Paola & Russ or Jenny & Sumit stretches feel flat with just level changes, the safer version is to stay minor-key trap and vary density instead of key: sparser and slower for emotional, busier and brighter for light. 160 credits each.
