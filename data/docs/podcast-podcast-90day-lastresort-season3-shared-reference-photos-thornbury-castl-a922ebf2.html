Thornbury Castle — real filming location, S3

90 Day: The Last Resort Season 3 was filmed at Thornbury Castle, Thornbury, South Gloucestershire, England — a real Grade I–listed Tudor castle (built 1511–1521 for the Duke of Buckingham; a Relais & Châteaux hotel since 1966). This is the actual building the couples stay in, so every establishing/location shot for S3 episodes should look like THIS castle, not a generic one.

Why this folder exists

The first pass of shared establishing stills each invented a different castle, because the prompts just said "castle." These reference photos lock all S3 location shots to the one real building.

The building's signature features (put these in every castle prompt)

  • Pale honey-grey ashlar (cut-stone) walls — Cotswold limestone, warm sandy tone.
  • Square crenellated battlements (notched parapets) along the roofline.
  • Round crenellated drum towers with battlemented tops.
  • Ornate red-brick TWISTED / spiralled Tudor chimneys in tall clustered stacks — the

castle's most distinctive detail (among the oldest ornate Tudor chimneys in England, c.1514). Red brick against the pale stone.

  • Large stone-mullioned oriel & bay windows (tall gridded leaded windows, some

projecting) — the Duke's chambers have the grandest ones.

  • Tudor four-centred (flattened) pointed-arch stone gateways, some with rusted iron

gates; partly-ruined ivy-clad outer walls.

  • Grounds: clipped tall yew hedges, walled Privy Garden, herbaceous flower borders

(pink/white/yellow), striped mown lawns, a 500-year-old vineyard; the tower of St Mary's parish church rises just beyond the walls.
