AI Voiceovers

Summary

A quick tutorial on generating AI voiceovers with ElevenLabs — which Anthony calls the #1 AI voice generation tool online.

Why use AI voiceovers

  • Client commercials: a professional voiceover delivers the marketing message without hiring/recording an on-camera actor and setting up microphones. It costs pennies but noticeably raises production quality. Anthony runs marketing for local businesses on retainer (e.g., a gym); because he already has tons of their footage, he makes new commercials by re-editing existing clips and adding a fresh AI voiceover (different "actor," different script). This lets him produce far more content with much less work — better ROI for clients without charging much more. (A gym commercial voiceover is played as an example.)
  • Faceless YouTube channels: AI voiceover + B-roll (AI or real). What matters is whether the content is valuable or entertaining, not that AI made it.
  • Book / audiobook narration for authors.
  • Online courses without being on camera: type each video's script, generate a voiceover, and pair it with simple PowerPoint slides.

Demo on PromptEdit

Audio tab → Voice → ElevenLabs TTS (text-to-speech). Paste your text into the box (he uses a segment from a course script). Select a voice from the library (play icon previews each; he picks "Jessica"). Adjust the Stability slider — low = expressive with energy fluctuation, high = more consistent/monotone; he usually sits around 0.1–0.4. Choose a different language if needed, leave text normalization on auto, and hit Generate. Preview the result, then Download the file to use however you like — the output is convincing enough that it's hard to tell it's AI.
