AI Voice Swap

Module: AI Video Creation · Instructor: Anthony · Length: ~4 min Lesson URL: https://www.contentcreator.com/products/ai-creator-course/categories/2157469701/posts/2197590890

Summary

How to use the ElevenLabs Voice Changer to swap the voice in an existing video for a completely different one. Builds directly on the AI Voiceovers and AI Voice Clone lessons.

Why swap voices

The main reason is consistency across clips that ended up with different-sounding voices. Anthony's example: a 3-clip AI demo generated in Kling 2.6. Kling produces audio, but each clip's voice came out slightly different, so the character sounds inconsistent when the clips play back to back. The voice changer replaces them all with one consistent voice.

Process

  1. Export just the audio from your editor. In Premiere Pro (CapCut/DaVinci also work): File → Export Media → change the format to MP3 → name it (e.g., "Original Audio") → Export.
  2. On PromptEdit → Audio tab → Voice → ElevenLabs (Voice Changer). Upload the original audio file.
  3. Under Target Voice, choose the replacement — your own voice clone or a preset (preview with the play button). He picks "Jessica" for a female character.
  4. Bring Stability to around 0.4 (this has a big impact), and leave "remove background noise" off for a cleaner, less-processed result. Hit Generate.
  5. The output plays with one consistent voice across the entire video.

Takeaway

It costs only a few pennies and has countless applications across AI content — most importantly, unifying inconsistent AI-generated voices into a single coherent voice.
