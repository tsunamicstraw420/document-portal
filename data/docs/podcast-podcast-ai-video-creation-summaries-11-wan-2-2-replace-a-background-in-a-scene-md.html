Overview

This lesson covers using Wan 2.2 Replace on PromptEdit.com to swap out the background of an existing video clip while keeping the subject (a person or character) in place, along with their motion. Rather than replacing the whole background in a still image and masking it in your editor (the manual approach taught in the AI Studio Background lesson), Wan 2.2 handles background replacement directly on the moving video.

Where it fits

It's the companion to the previous lesson (Wan 2.2 Replace Characters in a Scene) — same tool, applied to the background instead of the character. Together with Runway Aleph and Kling o1 Edit, it's part of this module's set of tools for editing existing footage (changing environments, characters, and effects) rather than generating clips from scratch.
