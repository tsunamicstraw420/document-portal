4. Exclusions (set these so you don't waste budget)

    Exclude your own subscribers — you're paying to reach new people, not re-serve fans.
    Exclude Kids content / "made for kids" placements.
    Consider excluding viewers under 18 (reality-TV-drama audience skews adult; tighter targeting).


5. Suggested budget & run settings

    Split: ~70% of budget on the Custom Segment (Section 1), ~30% on Affinity (Section 2).
    Duration: run at least 7–14 days so YouTube's system can optimize — very short bursts don't learn.
 Creative: promote a video with a strong hook in the first 5 seconds; subscriber conversion follows watch-through.
 Measure: watch subscribers-gained and cost-per-view, not just views, since the goal is channel growth.
