Read this first — two decisions baked into these prompts

  1. Likeness / style — important. These are real TLC cast members, and the press stills in cast_manifest.json are flagged editorial-tier, NOT owned. Making photoreal AI doubles of real private individuals for a monetized show is a rights-of-publicity and platform-policy risk. So every prompt below builds a stylized animated caricature — an "elevated cartoon," clearly not photoreal — that evokes each person through the traits Ruby names in the script (age, location, job, signature detail), not a realistic face-clone. The press stills are used only as your own on-screen editorial reference, not as a likeness target. If you'd rather go photoreal, that's a bigger legal conversation first.
  1. Ruby's canonical look is a placeholder. I don't have a locked visual of Ruby yet. The Ruby reference below is a first proposal — confirm/adjust hair, skin tone, age vibe, wardrobe and it becomes the fixed host avatar for every episode.

The cast cross-check is done: all five couples in the intro roll-call are covered (Marissa & Edward, Mallorie & Rasit, Ashia & Maxwell, Shea & Annabelle, Catie & Josh), plus the named secondary characters who get real beats. No one is skipped.
