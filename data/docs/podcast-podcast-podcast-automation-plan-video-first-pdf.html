 Section 2: The Video-First Philosophy
 The original plan was audio-first — upload to RSS.com, then distribute. This updated plan flips the priority:
 YouTube is your primary growth engine, and everything else (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, RSS.com) becomes
 secondary distribution that happens automatically in the background.
 Why? Video-first podcasts grow faster on YouTube's algorithm and Shorts than traditional podcast directories.
 Your audience is already watching content — meet them where they are. And because Ruby's voice is
 AI-narrated, you can produce episodes faster with no recording sessions.
