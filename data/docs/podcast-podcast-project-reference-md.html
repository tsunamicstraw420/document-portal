Production pipeline detail

Step 1 — Per-episode script (Claude drafts, Ruby refines)

Claude writes the production script (.txt) with production notes, delivery tags, timing markers. Claude then strips it into a clean teleprompter version. Ruby reviews and tweaks before reading.

Step 2 — Record narration (Wednesdays)

Ruby reads the teleprompter script and records her voice.

Step 3 — Build the show: Magnific → DaVinci Resolve (Wednesdays)

Magnific turns Ruby's voice into the animated show; in DaVinci Resolve, overlay reality TV images and clips, add captions, intro/outro, and music, then export. Publish to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube. Final assembly/polish in Resolve is a human editorial step.
