  SECTION 1 — Before You Start
 Before you press Record for the first time, make sure you have everything below. This is a one-time checklist —
 once it's done, you won't need to do it again.



 Your Equipment Checklist
  ■    A computer (Mac or Windows) with a stable internet connection

  ■    A USB microphone or headset with a built-in mic (any USB mic works — a Blue Yeti, Rode NT-USB, or
       even a basic headset is fine to start)

  ■    Headphones or earbuds (very important — prevents echo during guest recordings)

  ■    A webcam or built-in laptop camera (you'll record video too, not just audio)

  ■    A quiet room with the door closed (turn off fans, AC, TVs nearby)



 Your Account Checklist
  ■    Riverside.fm Pro account already created (Eddie set this up — login info is in the Accounts_info.rtf file)

  ■    You're using Google Chrome or Firefox browser (Riverside works best in Chrome)

  ■    You've allowed your browser to access your microphone and camera (Chrome will ask you — click Allow)


      QUICK TIP

      Always wear headphones when recording with a guest. If your speakers are on, the guest's voice will
      leak back into your microphone and cause an echo. Headphones = no echo.


      IMPORTANT

      Riverside records locally on your computer. This means the file is saved to your device in real time —
      which is great for quality. But it also means: DON'T close the browser tab while it says 'Uploading.' Wait
      until it says 'Ready.'

  SECTION 4 — Recording a Solo Episode
 A solo recording is just you — no guests. Perfect for hot takes, recaps, or any episode where it's just your voice.
 This is the simplest way to record. Follow these steps every Tuesday on recording day.



 Before You Hit Record — Your Pre-Show Routine:
  ■     Close all other apps and browser tabs (reduces computer slowdown)

  ■     Turn off or silence your phone

  ■     Close the door to your room

  ■     Put on your headphones

  ■     Have your show notes or talking points open (on your phone, printed, or on a second screen)

  ■     Get a glass of water nearby



 Step-by-Step: Recording Your Episode

    1      Go to riverside.fm and log in


    2      Click on your Studio
           'Reality Recaps Unfiltered' on the dashboard


    3      Click 'Enter Studio'
           A pre-recording setup screen appears — this is the 'lobby'


    4      Check your microphone
           In the lobby, look for the Microphone dropdown. Select your USB mic from the list. Speak and watch
           the audio level bar move — if it moves, your mic is working.


    5      Check your camera
           Select your camera from the Camera dropdown. You should see yourself on screen.


    6      Click 'Join Studio'
           You enter the studio. You'll see yourself in a video tile.


    7      Take a breath — you have 5 seconds
           When you click Record, a 5-second countdown appears. Use it to get comfortable.




Reality Recaps Unfiltered — Riverside.fm Recording Guide                                                       Page 5
    8      Click the red RECORD button
           It's the large button at the bottom center of the screen. The 5-second countdown begins.


    9      Start talking!
           When the countdown hits zero, you're recording. Don't worry about mistakes — you can edit them
           out later.


    10     When you're done — click STOP
           Click the Stop icon next to the Record button. A confirmation box will appear — click 'Stop
           Recording'.


    11     WAIT for the upload
           A progress bar will appear showing your file uploading. DO NOT close the browser until it disappears
           and says 'Ready'. This takes 2–10 minutes depending on your internet.


    12     You're done recording!
           Your episode is now saved in your project. Head to Section 6 for what to do next.



     PRO TIP — HANDLING MISTAKES

     If you make a mistake while recording — don't stop and restart. Just pause for 2 seconds, then say the
     sentence again clearly. The silence gap makes it easy to find and cut the mistake in editing later.


     IMPORTANT

     If your audio level bar is NOT moving when you speak, your microphone is not selected correctly. Go
     back to the Microphone dropdown and try a different option. Never start recording without confirming the
     level bar moves when you talk.

  SECTION 5 — Recording with a Guest
 Guests don't need a Riverside account. They just click a link you send them. Riverside records your voices
 separately — so even if your guest has shaky internet, their audio will still sound clear.



 How to Invite a Guest

    1      Enter your Studio
           Follow steps 1–6 from Section 4 to enter the studio.


    2      Find the Guest Link
           Inside the studio, look for a 'Invite' button or 'Copy Guest Link' option. Click it — the link copies to
           your clipboard automatically.


    3      Send the link to your guest
           Paste it in a text message, DM, or email. It looks like: riverside.fm/studio/xxxxxx/guest. Send it at
           least 15 minutes before your recording time.


    4      Ask your guest to use Chrome or Firefox
           Riverside works best in those browsers. Ask them NOT to use Safari.


    5      Wait for them to join
           When your guest clicks the link, they'll see a setup screen where they pick their mic and camera.
           Once they click 'Join Studio,' they appear in your video grid.


    6      Do a quick sound check
           Before recording, say 'Can you hear me okay? How does my audio sound?' Have them do the same.
           Check that both level bars are moving when each person speaks.


    7      Hit RECORD
           Same as Section 4 — click the red Record button, wait for the countdown, and go.



 What Your Guest Sees (Their Experience)
 Your guest gets a much simpler version of the studio — they can see you, hear you, and talk. They can't
 accidentally stop your recording or change any settings. You, as the host, are always in control.

  SECTION 6 — After Recording: AI Show Notes & Clips
 Once your recording is done and uploaded, Riverside's AI goes to work automatically. It transcribes everything
 you said, generates show notes, and finds the best soundbites for social media clips — all without you doing
 anything extra.



 How to Access Your AI Content
    1      From the dashboard, click your Studio


    2      Click on the recording/project — it'll be listed by date


    3      You'll see tabs across the top:
           Recordings | Transcript | Magic Clips | Show Notes | Edits | Exports



 The Transcript Tab
 Riverside automatically writes out everything that was said in your episode — word for word. This usually takes
 5–15 minutes after your upload finishes. It's very accurate (about 99% for clear speech). You can:

 • Read through and find any moments you want to cut
 • Search for any word or phrase (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F works in the transcript)
 • Download the full transcript as a text file



 Show Notes Tab — AI Writes Your Episode Description
 Click 'Generate Show Notes' and Riverside's AI reads your transcript and writes:

 • Episode title suggestions (pick your favorite)
 • Episode description (the text that goes on RSS.com and Spotify)
 • Chapter markers with timestamps (e.g., '3:42 — The Valley recap starts')
 • Key takeaways in bullet point form
 • Best quotes from the episode


     TIME SAVER

     Copy the AI-generated show notes and paste them directly into RSS.com when you publish. You'll still
     want to give it a quick read and tweak anything that sounds off — but it saves you 30+ minutes of writing
     every episode.

  SECTION 7 — Basic Editing
 You don't need to be a video editor. Riverside's editor works by reading your transcript — deleting a word from
 the text automatically cuts that moment from the audio and video. It's like editing a document.



 How to Open the Editor
    1      Go to your project page (your studio → click the recording)


    2      Click 'Edit' or 'Open Editor'


    3      The editor opens — you'll see your transcript on one side and a video preview on the other



 Removing 'Um,' 'Uh,' and Filler Words — One Click
    1      In the editor, look for 'Smooth Speech' or 'Remove Filler Words' in the toolbar


    2      Click it — a panel opens


    3      Choose 'Cut' (removes the filler word completely) or 'Mute' (silences it but keeps the timing)


    4      Click 'Apply' — Riverside automatically removes every 'um,' 'uh,' 'like,' and 'you know' in the
           recording


    5      Preview the result — if you removed something you liked, click Undo



 Removing Long Pauses and Silences
    1      In the transcript, silences appear as dots: . (short) .. (medium) ... (long)


    2      Click on a dot symbol → it highlights


    3      Press Backspace on your keyboard → that pause is removed


    4      Or: use 'Remove Silences' in the toolbar to remove all long pauses at once



 Cutting a Specific Mistake or Moment




Reality Recaps Unfiltered — Riverside.fm Recording Guide                                                   Page 11
    1      In the transcript, click on the word where the mistake starts


    2      Click and drag to highlight the words you want to remove


    3      Press Backspace — those words (and their audio/video) disappear


    4      The text turns gray with a strikethrough — it's hidden, not deleted. You can undo.



 Fixing Volume (Making Everyone Sound Equal)
    1      In the editor toolbar, find 'Normalize Audio Levels'


    2      Click it — Riverside automatically balances everyone's volumes


    3      No more 'turn it up, turn it down' for every speaker



     MINIMUM VIABLE EDIT

     You don't have to edit every single episode perfectly. At minimum: remove filler words with Smooth
     Speech, cut silences, and normalize audio. Those three things take less than 10 minutes and make a
     huge difference.


     IMPORTANT

     Always click 'Export' after editing to get the edited version. If you just close the editor, Riverside saves
     your edits — but you need to export to get the final file for publishing.

  SECTION 8 — Exporting & Publishing Your Episode
 Once you're happy with your edit, it's time to export the file and publish. You'll upload the audio to RSS.com
 (which sends it to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, etc.) and upload the video to YouTube.



 Step 1 — Export From Riverside
    1      In the editor, click the 'Export' button (top right corner)


    2      For RSS.com (audio podcast):
           Choose 'Audio' → Format: MP3. This is the file you'll upload to RSS.com.


    3      For YouTube (video podcast):
           Choose 'Video' → Quality: 1080p HD. This is the file you'll upload to YouTube.


    4      Click Export — Riverside processes the file


    5      When it's ready, click Download — the file saves to your computer's Downloads folder



 Step 2 — Upload to RSS.com (For Spotify, Apple Podcasts, etc.)
    1      Go to dashboard.rss.com and log in (credentials in Accounts_info.rtf)


    2      Click 'New Episode'


    3      Upload your MP3 file


    4      Fill in Episode Title — use the AI-generated title from Riverside's Show Notes


    5      Paste your Show Notes description into the episode description box


    6      Set your publish date and time (Fridays for Reality Recaps Unfiltered)


    7      Click Publish — RSS.com sends it to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Google Podcasts, and all other
           directories automatically



 Step 3 — Upload to YouTube
    1      Go to studio.youtube.com and log in



Reality Recaps Unfiltered — Riverside.fm Recording Guide                                                     Page 13
    2      Click 'Create' (top right corner) → 'Upload Video'


    3      Drag your MP4 file into the upload box, or click 'Select Files'


    4      While it uploads, fill in: Title (same as RSS.com), Description (same Show Notes), and Tags
           (Reality TV, Bravo, 90 Day Fiance, Reality Recaps Unfiltered)


    5      Add a Thumbnail — use the cover art or a screenshot from the episode


    6      Set visibility to 'Public'


    7      Click 'Save' or 'Publish'



     PUBLISHING ORDER

     Do RSS.com first, then YouTube. RSS goes faster (audio file only). While YouTube is uploading the
     video (which takes longer), you can be doing other things — the upload continues in the background.

  SECTION 10 — Using the Riverside iPhone App
 Riverside has a free iPhone app that lets you record, join sessions as a guest, and use your phone as a second
 camera. It's not a replacement for your laptop — but it's incredibly handy to know when and how to use it.



 Download the App
    1       Open the App Store on your iPhone


    2       Search: Riverside Podcast Video Studio


    3       Tap 'Get' to download — it's free


    4       Open the app and log in with the same email and password you use on the website


    5       Your studios and recordings will appear automatically — they sync from the web



 When To Use the iPhone App
 The iPhone app is great for specific situations. For your regular Tuesday recordings, always use your laptop.
 Here's when the phone becomes your best tool:

   Device                   Situation

   USE iPHONE               Recording a quick bonus episode or hot take on the go

   USE iPHONE               Joining someone else's Riverside session as a guest

   USE iPHONE               Adding a second camera angle to your laptop recording

   USE iPHONE               Recording vertical/portrait video for Reels or TikTok clips

   USE iPHONE               Your guest is joining from their phone (they use it automatically)

   USE LAPTOP               Your main weekly Tuesday episode — access to all AI and editing tools

   USE LAPTOP               Downloading and exporting your finished episode files

   USE LAPTOP               Editing transcripts, show notes, and Magic Clips

   USE LAPTOP               Live fan call-in segments

   USE LAPTOP               Anything that requires the full Riverside editor

  SECTION 9 — Quick Troubleshooting
 Something not working? Check here first before panicking.


   Problem                                      Solution

   My mic isn't being picked up                 In the studio lobby, click the Microphone dropdown and select a different
                                                option. Speak and watch the audio level bar — keep switching until the bar
                                                moves.

   I can't see myself on camera                 Click the Camera dropdown and select your webcam. If nothing works, close
                                                Chrome, unplug your camera, plug it back in, and reopen Riverside.

   My guest can't join                          Make sure you sent them the Guest Link (not just the main URL). Ask them to
                                                use Chrome and to click 'Allow' when their browser asks for mic/camera
                                                permission.

   There's an echo during the session           Someone's speakers are on instead of headphones. You and your guest
                                                should both wear headphones or earbuds. Headphones = no echo.

   The recording says 'Failed' or               Check your internet connection. Open a new tab and try loading a website. If
   didn't upload                                internet works, go back to your project in Riverside — there's usually a cloud
                                                backup (lower quality) that saved automatically.

   I accidentally closed the browser            Reopen Riverside and go to your project. Riverside saves a cloud backup in
   while uploading                              addition to the local recording. You may lose some quality but you won't lose
                                                the episode.

   The transcript has wrong words               This is normal for names, slang, and show-specific terms. Click on the wrong
                                                word in the transcript and type the correct word. Changes only affect the
                                                transcript text — not the audio.

   Riverside is frozen or loading               Press F5 (or Cmd+R on Mac) to refresh the page. If still frozen, close Chrome
   forever                                      completely, reopen it, and go back to riverside.fm.



     IMPORTANT

     If Riverside is acting strange, the first fix is almost always: refresh the page (press F5 or Cmd+R), or
     close Chrome and reopen it. 90% of issues fix themselves this way.
