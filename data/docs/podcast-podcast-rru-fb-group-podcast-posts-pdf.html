     How to use these (so it never feels like spam)
     • Rotate them. Never post the same one twice in a row. One every few days beats one
     every day.
     • Put the link in the FIRST COMMENT, not the post. Facebook quietly shows
     link-posts to fewer people, and “link’s in the comments” reads friendlier anyway.
     • Reply to everyone who comments. In a group, the conversation is the promotion —
     the post is just the door.
     • Spread them across the groups. A different post in each group the same week, so
     no one who’s in two of them sees a copy-paste.
     • Read the room. If a group’s been quiet or someone just posted something heavy, sit
     a day out.
