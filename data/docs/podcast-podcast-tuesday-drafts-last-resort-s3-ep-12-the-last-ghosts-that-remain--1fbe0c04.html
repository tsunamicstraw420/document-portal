>>> SEGMENT 1 · 1:35 — LEAD: Rebecca & Zied (the medium)
// PRODUCTION NOTE // Candlelit reading footage. Fair-read counterargument AND the arc callback
// both live here — do not cut either. B4 quote bubble on 'she has already made up her mind.'

[leaning in]
Let's start with the séance. Rebecca and Zied.

Rebecca has been carrying this suspicion for weeks — that Zied has a whole second life running somewhere she can't see. *** And the retreat is nearly over, and she has not gotten an answer she believes. *** So she goes and asks a medium. ***

[dry] Not the therapists. *** Not Zied. *** A medium. *** In the castle. *** With the candles. ***

And I need to be careful here, because I do not make fun of what people believe. *** Plenty of people find real clarity in a reading and I'm not stepping on that. *** But watch what she is actually doing in that room. *** She is not asking for guidance. *** She is asking for a verdict. ***

[fair]
Now let me give Rebecca the fair read, because she absolutely deserves one. *** This is a woman who has spent this entire season asking her husband direct questions and getting fog back. *** When the person in front of you won't give you a straight answer, you will take an answer from anywhere. *** A friend. *** A group chat. *** A stranger with tarot cards. *** That's not silly. *** That's what desperate looks like when it runs out of doors. ***

[dry] But here's the thing. *** You do not go to a psychic when you want information. *** You go when you want permission. *** Rebecca has already made up her mind about Zied. *** She is out here shopping for a second opinion that agrees with the first one. ***

[direct]
And go back to last week. *** The puppet show fallout — where these two stopped speaking, and Rebecca said flat out she should not have to be the one to apologize. *** That standoff never broke. *** It just went underground. *** And now instead of turning to her husband, she is turning to the spirit world. *** That's not a marriage in trouble. *** That is a marriage where the two people have stopped using each other as the source. ***
