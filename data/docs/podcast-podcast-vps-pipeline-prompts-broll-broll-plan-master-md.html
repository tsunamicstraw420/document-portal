COPYRIGHT SAFETY — non-negotiable (this is a MONETIZED channel)

Choose source in this priority order (safest first). Default to the top of the list; only descend when a specific face/moment truly requires it:

  1. artlist_ai (generative video) or artlist_stock / pexels / pixabay (royalty-free stock) → safety_level: "safe". Best for emotional/dramatic beats. Describe the feeling, not the actual scene. This is the default for most cues.
  2. graphic (animated text, quote card, lower-third, timeline) → safety_level: "safe". Great for quotes, "the moment" reveals, recaps of who-did-what.
  3. cast_photo (a named press still from the CAST LIBRARY, Ken Burns + text) → safety_level: "caution". Use ONLY when a specific couple/cast member must be shown. These are editorial-tier press stills, NOT owned — always transform (Ken Burns + caption). Must set cast_file to an exact path from the CAST LIBRARY block.
  4. actual_clip (real show footage) → safety_level: "high_risk". {{allow_actual_clips_line}} Even when allowed: <10s, heavily transformed, criticism/commentary only, never the "heart" of a scene. Always provide a strong alt_safe_option.

Score every cue against the four fair-use factors (purpose, nature, amount, market effect). Reality TV is highly protected and Content-ID-aggressive in 2026 — make Ruby's commentary the star; visuals only support it. When in doubt, pick generative/stock and go MORE specific in the prompt (age, wardrobe color, exact emotion, lighting, camera move).

CUE RULES

  • Anchor accuracy: anchor_quote MUST be copied verbatim from the transcript (contiguous words, exact spelling/punctuation as shown). Do not paraphrase — the matcher fails on paraphrase and falls back to a nearby sentence.
  • Density: aim for about {{target_broll_count}} cues total, spread across the episode. Leave at least {{min_gap_seconds}} seconds between consecutive anchors. Front-load nothing; cover the whole runtime.
  • cue_type mapping: generative/stock → usually clip (or image for a still); cast press still → image; text/quote card → graphic; music/sting → music (rare — Ruby adds most music in DaVinci Resolve).
  • Cast beats: if the anchor names a couple/cast member who is in the CAST LIBRARY and the beat needs their face, use source: cast_photo + the exact cast_file. Otherwise prefer stock/generative that evokes the emotion.
  • Duration: 4–8s for stills, 6–15s for clips; never > 18s.
  • Do the reasoning first (identify the richest emotional beats), then emit ONLY the JSON.
