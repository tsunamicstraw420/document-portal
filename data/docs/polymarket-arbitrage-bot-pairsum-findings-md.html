Findings: the guru wallet's real edge is pair-sum market-making, not latency

Date: 2026-06-11 Wallet investigated: 0xb945945d5bcaf7b56834d4da8cdf8f8f94b2db68 (the @0xSurferX funnel "proof" wallet, profile @l5zn1bwom8etsk, +$20,945 all-time verified on its Polymarket P/L chart). The one-line answer

On BTC 15-min Up/Down markets, predicting direction is dead, but providing liquidity to both sides is alive. The guru's profit is 100% the second thing.

How we know — the decomposition

Every window's profit splits exactly into two parts:

  • ARB — the shares where he holds UP and DOWN equally. This part pays out no matter which way Bitcoin moves, so its profit is locked and equals how far under $1 he bought the pair.
  • DIRECTIONAL — the leftover imbalance, a pure bet on which way BTC goes.

The arb part is positive on both days, including the day he lost money overall. The directional part is negative on both days. His losses are his direction bets; his profits are his liquidity provision.

Why this reconciles everything we measured

  • Our own 20,000-sample calibration backtest: buying across the price curve and holding to resolution nets −2.16¢ per $1 for a taker (you pay the ask + the 1.7¢ fee). The BTC-15min market is extremely well-calibrated — a token priced at P wins ~P% of the time.
  • Our latency probe: Polymarket reprices within 0.25s of Binance — latency arb is dead.
  • Both are true and the guru profits, because he is a maker, not a taker. Resting limit bids fill at the bid (you collect the spread instead of paying it) and at the reduced maker fee. That swing is the entire gap between our −2.16¢ and his win.

The mechanism (how he ladders)

  • 3,500 buys, 0 sells — pure buy-and-hold to resolution, never trades out.
  • Tiny orders: $5 median, $30 max — hundreds of small resting bids per window (~83 fills/window).
  • Both sides at once, rested slightly below the running price. As the 15-min price oscillates, his bids get hit on the dips, so each side's average cost lands below the mid. Combined, his average pair costs 94¢ (37 of 41 windows under $1). That ~6¢ under par, on the hedged shares, is the locked profit.
  • Spread across the whole price curve, weighted to the cheap end (27% of shares at 0–20¢). ~83% hedged.

What this means for us

  • Build a pair-sum maker, not a directional bot. A disciplined version could beat the guru: he bleeds −$1,015 letting his book drift directional; a bot that actively stays hedged captures the +$1,555 arb and skips that drag.
  • This is a different bot from the latency arb — it holds both sides and must keep inventory balanced.

The make-or-break unknown

The entire edge depends on our resting bids getting filled at something near his rate. Our Path-A logger measured maker fill rates at 0.7–2%; he overcomes that with sheer order volume. Whether our infrastructure can — under Polymarket's order rate limits, clearing the April-2026 maker fee (reduced + rebate, not zero) — is unknown and is exactly what a dry-run must measure before any capital is committed.

Note on the dry-run wallet: the arb wallet has been emptied. A simulated dry-run (watch the live book, model which of our hypothetical bids would fill) needs no funds and is the safe first measurement. A true live-fill test eventually needs a small USDC balance, because a real resting order needs collateral to rest and fill.
