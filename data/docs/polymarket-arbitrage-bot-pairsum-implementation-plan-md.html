1. The goal & the proven edge

Replicate — faithfully, not approximately — the guru wallet 0xb945945d5bcaf7b56834d4da8cdf8f8f94b2db68 (+$20,945 all-time, verified). Its edge, decomposed from 3,500 real fills:

  • ARB component +$1,555 (balanced both-sides pairs, direction-independent)
  • DIRECTIONAL component −$1,015 (the unmatched imbalance — he eats it, out-volumes it)
  • Net +$540 / 58 windows ≈ +$9/window on ~$340 deployed/window (~2.6% gross/window, high variance)

The edge is maker market-making: rest bids on both UP and DOWN across the whole price curve; buy the pair for an average ~94¢; collect $1.00 at resolution. It is NOT directional and NOT latency.

Standing rule for this build: copy the guru's measured method exactly. The ONLY permitted deviation is capital scale, applied as proportional scaling — never a redesign. No improvised variants (no chasing, no flatten exit, no "balanced-only entry", no "cut the side") without first proving they beat the guru.

3. Capital requirement — the hard floor

A faithful carpet has a fixed cost. Resting 5-share (the exchange minimum) bids at 1¢ spacing across 0.05–0.95 on both sides:

cost ≈ 5 shares × Σ(prices 0.05..0.95) × 2 sides ≈ 5 × 45.5 × 2 ≈ $455

Plus filled bids become positions held to resolution (~15–20 min) → capital stays tied up until auto-redeem. So working capital ≈ the full carpet cost.

4. The bot — what's built vs. what's left

Base file: pairsum_test.py (already on the box + in repo). Already built & verified:

  • ✅ No-chase static ladder (anchored once/window, never chases) — the v1→v2 fix
  • ✅ Hold to resolution (no flatten) + auto-redeem — the guru's exit
  • ✅ Partial-fill tracking (polls each rung to full; records incremental deltas)
  • ✅ Graceful shutdown (SIGTERM cancels unfilled, holds filled — no orphans)
  • ✅ Balance stop, live state feed + web cockpit (pairsum_cockpit.py, :8090)
  • ✅ Fee model understood (maker 0%), redemption verified

To build for faithful replication (the deltas):

  1. Full-curve carpet ladder. Replace the 3-rung [0.03,0.07,0.12]-below-mid ladder with bids at every 1¢ from PMIN→PMAX (0.05→0.95) on both sides, placed once at window open, held. Config: SPACING=0.01, PMIN=0.05, PMAX=0.95.
  2. Capital-aware carpet builder. At window start, compute the carpet's total cost; if it exceeds the per-window budget, trim to fit without breaking faithfulness order-of-preference: (a) skip the most expensive high-price rungs first, then (b) widen spacing to 2¢, then (c) narrow the range. Log exactly what was trimmed (no silent caps).
  3. Order size by capital. SHARES = 5 at the $500 tier; scale toward 10–20 as capital grows. Keep ≥5 (exchange minimum).
  4. Inventory / exposure cap. Track total committed (resting bids + held positions); stop placing new bids when within a margin of WORKING_CAPITAL. Prevents over-commit on a fast-filling trend.
  5. Drawdown kill. Halt if wallet balance < START_BAL × (1 − MAX_DD), MAX_DD = 0.20. Protects the bankroll from a bad run (the directional drag is real and will produce losing stretches).
  6. PnL = wallet truth. Session PnL from real balance deltas (already wired); the cockpit's cumulative-PnL line is the verdict, NOT the combined-entry metric (which only measures the good half).

6. Rollout sequence

  1. Dry-run (no --live): confirm the carpet builds correctly, fits budget, and the cockpit renders the full ladder. Zero capital.
  2. Fund ~$1,000 (or min $500) to the arb wallet 0x06b4e8aa…ae204.
  3. Go live, one window at a time, 5-share orders. Watch the cockpit.
  4. Measure over ≥50 windows (≈1–2 days): fills/window (target → 35–70), combined entry (target → 0.94), % two-sided windows, and above all the wallet balance trend.
  5. Scale up only if the wallet trend is positive over that real sample. Increase order size / overlap windows toward guru density.
  6. If the wallet trend is negative over 50+ faithful windows → that is a real verdict (the strongest version tested), and we stop.

7. Honest expectations & kill criteria

  • This is a thin-edge, high-variance grind: ~6¢/pair, only meaningful across hundreds of windows. Expect losing stretches — the directional drag (−$1,015 in the guru's own data) is inherent; the arb only out-earns it on volume.
  • Realistic upside at $1,000: if we hit ~half the guru's density and his per-pair edge, low-single-digit-% per day, swingy. Not a money printer — a grind.
  • Maker rebate (~20% of taker fees) is a small tailwind on top.
  • Kill criteria: wallet down −20% (auto), OR negative wallet trend across 50+ faithful (carpeted, ≥$500) windows. Below $200 capital: don't start at all.
  • The combined-entry number on the cockpit will look rosy even when losing — judge by the wallet, not the dashboard.
