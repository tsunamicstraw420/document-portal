Empirical findings from today

  1. Bot's BUY log price field was lying. Recorded "fill=$0.58" when actual on-chain fill was $0.03. Root cause: _actual_fill_price fell back to ceiling because get_order doesn't expose a fill-price field. Fixed by switching to get_trades(asset_id=...) keyed by taker_order_id. The price field there is the actual avg fill.
  2. WS view can diverge from live book by ~$0.50. The 5/15 13:52 trade: bot's WS said ask=$0.525, live book had ask=$0.03 (market already priced UP near zero). The bot fired and lost $0.66. Fixed by tightening preflight to abort when |ws_ask − live_ask| > $0.10.
  3. Live-mode balance was oscillating. record_open deducted immediately, on-chain sync overwrote 60s later, no-fill refund credited back — heartbeat reported false +N then -N then 0. Fixed by making record_open not deduct in live mode; available_balance (= current − reserved_usdc) is used for sizing instead.
  4. Phantom fills were a real phenomenon. status='matched' + size_matched=0 on immediate readback was the bot's race-condition signature. The old "refund and remove position" path created untracked on-chain positions. Fixed by retrying get_order 4× with backoff before going OPTIMISTIC (assume fill, Telegram alert).
  5. Chainlink-settle hasn't filled once. 83 BUYs across May 11-15: 66 from Chainlink path, 16 from Binance. Every confirmed fill was Binance. The dispatch is now commented out; RTDS WS stays subscribed but doesn't influence trades.
  6. Wallet truth ≠ bot truth. The Phase C audit in the digest now reads data-api.polymarket.com/activity?user=PROXY directly. 48h window currently shows: 3 resolved trades (W:2 L:1), avg entry 35¢, win-rate 67%, realized PnL +$12.02.
