Automated Trading System(s) Implementation Plan

Project: Build automated trading system(s) based on ZCT trading frameworks Date Created: May 9, 2026 Based On: 5 trading strategy PDFs + industry best practices research

Executive Summary

This plan outlines the development of an automated trading system that implements Koroush AK's proven trading methodologies (momentum and mean reversion strategies) while maintaining human oversight and the critical psychological components that drive trader success.

The system will operate across three core modules aligned with the "Three Dimensions of Trading Performance":

  1. Strategy Module - Signal generation, edge detection, setup identification
  2. Risk Module - Position sizing, trade management, capital preservation
  3. Psyche Module - Logging, analysis, behavioral monitoring

The system will be progressively automated rather than fully autonomous, respecting the reality that 60-70% of trading success depends on disciplines that cannot be fully automated (psychology, routine, adaptability).

II. Module 1: Strategy Module (Signal Generation)

Purpose

Identify high-probability trading setups based on momentum and mean reversion frameworks.

Submodules

A. Market Structure Detection

Input: Raw OHLC + Volume data Output: Identified support/resistance levels, market regime

Implementation:

  • Daily timeframe: Identify key S/R zones (ZCT Framework)
  • 4-hour: Secondary levels and order flow
  • Hourly/15m: Entry-level precision and spike detection
  • Classify levels: Fresh (untested) vs Recycled (retested)

Rules:

  • Support: Price previously bounced up from this level
  • Resistance: Price previously bounced down from this level
  • Fresh levels: Higher probability, less noise
  • Recycled levels: More tests = stronger confirmation

B. VWAP-Based Trend Identification

Input: Price and Volume data Output: Current market regime (momentum vs reversion)

Implementation:

IF VWAP slope is STEEP and price is ABOVE VWAP:
  → Momentum environment → Favor BREAKOUT trades
  
IF VWAP slope is FLAT or choppy around VWAP:
  → Reversion environment → Favor REVERSAL trades
  
IF VWAP slope is STEEP and price is BELOW VWAP:
  → Momentum environment → Favor BREAKDOWN trades

C. Spike Detection Engine

Input: Intrabar price action, volume Output: Identified spikes with classification (fast vs slow)

Implementation:

FAST SPIKE: >2% move in <5 minutes with volume concentration
  → High probability reversion signal
  → Setup: Short into resistance after fast spike
  → Or: Long into support after fast spike
  
SLOW GRIND: Steady directional move over 15+ minutes
  → Momentum continuation signal
  → Setup: Long breakout of resistance in uptrend
  → Or: Short breakdown of support in downtrend

D. Signal Generation Rules

MOMENTUM (BREAKOUT) SIGNALS:

Buy Setup:
- Price above VWAP with steep upward slope
- Approaching or breaking above resistance level
- Volume increasing
- VWAP bands expanding upward
→ Entry: On breakout candle close above resistance
→ Stop: Below recent swing low or support
→ Target: Next resistance or 2:1 R:R

Sell Setup:
- Price below VWAP with steep downward slope  
- Approaching or breaking below support level
- Volume increasing
- VWAP bands expanding downward
→ Entry: On breakdown candle close below support
→ Stop: Above recent swing high or resistance
→ Target: Next support or 2:1 R:R

MEAN REVERSION (REVERSAL) SIGNALS:

Short Setup (Price spikes into resistance):
- Price makes fast spike above resistance
- VWAP flat/below price = weak trend
- Momentum traders just entered
- Weakness signals: Lower volume on spike, failure to break higher
→ Entry: Limit order at resistance level or on first rejection
→ Stop: Above recent highs (above resistance)
→ Target: Back to VWAP or support level at 2:1 R:R

Long Setup (Price dumps into support):
- Price makes fast dump below support
- VWAP flat/above price = weak trend
- Bears just panicked
- Weakness signals: Lower volume on dump, failure to break lower
→ Entry: Limit order at support level or on first bounce
→ Stop: Below recent lows (below support)
→ Target: Back to VWAP or resistance level at 2:1 R:R

III. Module 2: Risk Module (Capital Preservation)

Purpose

Ensure trades are sized and managed to protect capital and maintain sustainable growth.

Submodules

A. Position Sizing Engine

Fixed Risk Model:

Account Size: $10,000
Max Risk Per Trade: 1% = $100
Max Risk Per Day: 2% = $200
Max Risk Per Week: 5% = $500

For each trade:
- Distance to stop loss = SL (pips/points)
- Position Size = ($100 / SL) = Number of contracts/shares
- Example: If SL is 50 pips, position = 2 contracts (risking $100)

Scaling Rules:

  • Starting Level 2 traders: 0.5-1% risk per trade
  • Level 3 traders: 1-1.5% risk per trade (proven edge)
  • Level 4 traders: 1.5-2% risk per trade (full optimization)

B. Stop Loss & Take Profit Placement

For Momentum (Breakout) Trades:

Entry: Above resistance
Stop Loss: Below recent swing low (structure-based)
Take Profit: Next resistance or 2:1 R:R
Example: 
- Entry at 100.50 (above resistance at 100)
- Stop at 99.00 (below swing low at 99.20)
- Risk = 1.50 points
- Profit = 3.00 points (2:1 R:R)

For Mean Reversion (Reversal) Trades:

Entry: At/near resistance/support
Stop Loss: Above/below the level that was spiked (invalidation)
Take Profit: Back to VWAP or opposite level (2:1 R:R)
Example:
- Entry at 100.00 (resistance, after spike)
- Stop at 100.50 (above the spike high)
- Target: 99.50 (back toward support, 2:1 R:R)

C. Trade Management Engine

Real-time Monitoring:

  • Adverse price movement tracking
  • Daily/weekly cumulative loss monitoring
  • Position heat (profit/loss in real-time)
  • Time-based exits if setup fails

Automatic Actions:

  • Close trade if daily loss limit (-2%) is reached
  • Close trade if weekly loss limit (-5%) is reached
  • Move stop to break-even if trade moves 1:1 into profit
  • Partial profit-taking at 1:1 R:R (take 50%), let rest run

D. Performance Metrics Engine

Tracked Metrics:

  • Win rate (%)
  • Loss rate (%)
  • Average winner size
  • Average loser size
  • Reward:Risk ratio (R:R)
  • Profit factor (Gross Profit / Gross Loss)
  • Sharpe Ratio (Risk-adjusted returns)
  • Maximum Drawdown (%)
  • Payoff Ratio (Average Winner / Average Loser)

Optimization Feedback:

  • Monthly review of metrics
  • Identify which setups have highest R:R
  • Eliminate setups with <1.5:1 R:R
  • Increase frequency of high-R:R setups

IV. Module 3: Psyche Module (Human Factors & Logging)

Purpose

Maintain discipline, capture emotional data, and ensure sustainable trading behavior.

Submodules

A. Pre-Trade Checklist Automation

Before every trade entry, system prompts:

□ Is the setup clearly meeting the criteria?
□ Is position sizing correct for my account?
□ Is the stop loss properly placed?
□ Am I in a favorable market environment?
□ Have I checked my daily/weekly loss limits?
□ Am I in the right emotional state to trade?

System Action: 
- Blocks trade execution if checklist incomplete
- Records deviation if checklist skipped

B. Trade Logging System

Automatic capture (triggered at entry):

  • Entry price, time, direction
  • Position size and risk amount
  • Stop loss and take profit levels
  • Chart screenshot (for pattern recognition)
  • Market regime (momentum/reversion/choppy)
  • Trade setup classification

Manual capture (trader input):

  • Setup rationale (why this trade)
  • Confidence level (1-10)
  • Emotional state before entry (calm/greed/fear)
  • News/events that might affect trade

Auto-capture at exit:

  • Exit price, time, P&L
  • Hit stop vs hit profit target vs time exit
  • Actual return vs expected return
  • Duration of trade

C. Journal Analytics

Pattern Recognition:

  • When do trades succeed? (specific times, setups, conditions)
  • When do trades fail? (specific times, setups, conditions)
  • Best performing setup type (momentum vs reversion)
  • Time of day performance
  • Day of week patterns
  • Asset-specific performance

Behavioral Tracking:

  • Trades taken with high confidence vs low confidence
  • Emotional state impact on win rate
  • Deviation from plan tracking
  • Largest losses correlation (panic? greed? overleverage?)

D. Daily/Weekly Discipline Tracking

Daily Ritual Monitoring:

  • Pre-market routine completion (journal review, plan setup)
  • Trading hours adherence (only trade planned hours)
  • Post-trade review completion (log all trades)
  • Emotion check-in (daily mood log)

Weekly Review Automation:

  • Generate weekly stats report
  • Identify best setups
  • Identify worst setups
  • Calculate if metrics improved vs prior week
  • Suggest plan adjustments based on data

VIII. Risk Mitigation Strategies

Strategy Risk

  • Risk: System misses valid setups
  • Mitigation: Weekly manual backtesting, validation against missed trades
  • Response: Adjust signal thresholds, add additional signal confirmation

Execution Risk

  • Risk: System executes at wrong price or size
  • Mitigation: All trades require human confirmation in Phase 1-3
  • Response: Implement order validation checks, circuit breakers

Data Risk

  • Risk: Market data gaps or incorrect data
  • Mitigation: Multi-source data validation, data quality monitoring
  • Response: Automatic alerts if data integrity fails

Capital Risk

  • Risk: Losing more than configured risk limits
  • Mitigation: Hard position size limits, daily loss limits, circuit breakers
  • Response: System automatically halts trading if limits exceeded

Psychological Risk

  • Risk: Trader overrides system, abandons plan during drawdown
  • Mitigation: Daily discipline tracking, behavioral monitoring
  • Response: Alerts when deviation detected, forced review periods

XII. Critical Success Factors

  1. Respect the Three Dimensions: Don't over-automate psyche. Some decisions require human judgment.
  1. Start with Setups You Understand: Only automate rules you've personally proven work.
  1. Measure Everything: If you can't measure it, you can't optimize it.
  1. Gradual Automation: Phase in automation slowly. Maintain human control initially.
  1. Discipline Over Prediction: Focus on following rules, not on predicting the market.
  1. Edge Validation: Regularly validate that your edge still exists (market conditions change).
  1. Risk First: All other metrics matter only if capital is preserved.
