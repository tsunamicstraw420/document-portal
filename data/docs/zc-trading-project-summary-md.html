Key Insights from Analysis

The Three Dimensions of Trading Performance

Every successful trading system must address:

  1. Strategy (Technical/Analytical)
  • Journal: Document ALL trades with context
  • Edge: Identify YOUR specific market edge
  • Asset Selection: Trade the right instruments
  1. Risk (Quantitative/Capital Preservation)
  • Position Sizing: Risk % converted to contracts
  • Trade Management: Stop loss & profit target logic
  • Scaling: Sustainable account growth rules
  1. Psyche (Behavioral/Discipline)
  • Psychology: Emotional management
  • Routines: Pre-trade rituals
  • Discipline: Executing plan under pressure

Critical Insight: Weakness in ANY dimension prevents profitability scaling. All three must be addressed.

Two Core Trading Strategies

Momentum (Breakout)

  • Trade breakouts of resistance (long) and support (short)
  • Thesis: Strength continues
  • Trigger: Price above VWAP with steep slope + volume
  • Environment: Strong trends

Mean Reversion (Reversal)

  • Short spikes into resistance, long dumps into support
  • Thesis: Price reverts to average
  • Trigger: Fast spike with weakness signals
  • Environment: Range-bound, choppy markets
  • Core: Counter-trading momentum traders when they're weak

Market Regime Detection (VWAP)

  • Position vs VWAP: Shows trend direction
  • Slope of VWAP: Shows trend strength (steep = momentum, flat = reversion)
  • Bands: Show volatility and extremes

Critical Success Factors

  1. Respect the Three Dimensions: Don't over-automate psyche. Some decisions require human judgment.
  1. Start Simple, Validate Often: Build features only after backtesting proves they add value.
  1. Human Oversight is Mandatory: No fully autonomous execution. All trades require trader confirmation (Phases 1-3).
  1. Measure Everything: If you can't measure it, you can't optimize it.
  1. Discipline Over Prediction: Focus on following rules, not predicting the market.
  1. Edge Validation: Regularly validate your edge still exists as markets change.
  1. Risk First: All other metrics are secondary to capital preservation.

Important Notes

Philosophical Approach

This system is designed to be augmentation, not automation. The system amplifies human expertise through technology, not replace human judgment.

Key Principles:

  • System identifies setups → Trader confirms
  • System calculates sizing → Trader approves
  • System logs trades → Trader reviews
  • System suggests optimizations → Trader validates on backtest

Risk Management Philosophy

  1. First rule: Don't lose money
  2. Second rule: See rule 1
  3. Third rule: Never risk more than configured limits
  4. Fourth rule: Scale only after proving consistency

Trading Philosophy

Based on Koroush AK's proven frameworks:

  • Trade momentum in strong trends
  • Trade mean reversion in choppy markets
  • Always know your edge (why this trade works)
  • Always journal for continuous improvement
  • Discipline and consistency matter more than prediction

Questions or Issues?

When you're unsure about something, ask using this framework:

Trading Context: Is this momentum or reversion? How does it fit the Three Dimensions?

Validation: Has this been backtested? What was the accuracy?

Risk: Does this affect position sizing or risk limits?

Human Oversight: Who makes the final decision - human or system?

Measurement: How will we know if this improvement works?
