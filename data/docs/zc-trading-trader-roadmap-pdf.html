Level 0: No Strategy

                                     This is where every trader starts.


And where many stay longer than they realise...



You know you're Level 0 if:

• No strategy. Just tips and 'gut feelings'

• No written rules for entries, exits, or stop losses

• No journal. No screenshots. No data.

• Position sizes swing wildly (1% one day, 10% the next)

• Wins feel like skill. Losses feel like bad luck.



What's required to reach Level 1

                                The goal at Level 0 isn't to find a strategy.


It's to build three habits: a routine, a journal, and the resilience to keep showing up.



Strategy:
Start journaling every trade immediately after you close it to capture your entries, exits, trade
screenshots and emotional state.


IMPORTANT: Your journal is the single most important tool you'll ever use at ANY level as a trader.
Without this, there is no data... and without data, you can never improve.



Risk:
Choose a fixed position size you'll use on every trade. This removes the psyche component from
sizing and forces consistency.



Psyche:
Build a pre-trade routine. Before you enter any trade, do the same thing every time. This creates
muscle memory and reduces emotional decisions.
Level 1: Inconsistent Strategy

            You've built a strategy. You're journaling. But the results are all over the place.



You know you're Level 1 if:

• You have a written strategy with rules

• You journal your trades consistently

• You take the same position size most of the time

• But... some days you follow the rules. Other days you skip them or modify them

• Your win rate swings from 30% to 70%

• You can't tell if your strategy works or if you're just lucky



The bottleneck at Level 1 is Strategy.

You have a strategy, but you don't have the strategy for you. You're still tinkering, tweaking, switching
timeframes, adding indicators, and seeking the holy grail.



What's required to reach Level 2:

1. Commit to ONE strategy for at least 100 live trades. Not 10. Not 50. 100. This is
non-negotiable.

2. Document every trade. Why you entered. Why you exited. What worked. What didn't.

3. Analyze your journal for patterns. Look for what's working in your edge and what's holding you
back.
Level 2: Consistent Strategy

      You've proven your strategy works. You execute it with discipline. Your win rate is stable.



You know you're Level 2 if:

• You follow your rules 90%+ of the time

• Your win rate is consistent (55-60%, for example)

• You can explain your edge in one sentence

• You're journaling everything

• But... you're not profitable yet. Or barely breaking even



The bottleneck at Level 2 is Risk.

Your strategy is solid. But your risk management isn't. You're losing too much on bad trades. Your
winners don't outweigh your losers. Your position sizing, stop placement, or profit targets need work.



What's required to reach Level 3:

1. Implement a risk management framework. Risk per trade, risk per day, risk per week.

2. Optimize your position sizing. If you win 55% but make money, your R:R is good. If you win 60%
but lose money, it's bad.

3. Test different stop loss placements and profit targets. Find what works for your strategy and
your psychology.
